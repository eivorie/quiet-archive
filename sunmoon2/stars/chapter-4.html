Chapter IV

Home

Saturn △ Moon Orb · 0°29

Saturn is often misunderstood.

People speak of limits. Of distance. Of responsibility.

Yet Saturn also governs everything that remains after time has tested it.

The things that hold.

The things that stay.

In our synastry, Saturn doesn't arrive alone.

It reaches the Moon almost perfectly, with an orb of only twenty-nine arc minutes.

Emotion meets permanence.

Vulnerability meets structure.

It is not the kind of aspect that burns brightly for a moment.

It is the kind that quietly builds somewhere to return to.

"She stays."

The Fourth House speaks of something even deeper.

Home.

Not the place with walls.

The place where we no longer need to perform.

Where silence is allowed.

Where tiredness doesn't have to be hidden.

Where love is measured less by grand declarations than by quiet certainty.

Astrology suggests that Saturn touched that place.

Not to make it heavier.

But to make it last.

Some people become memories.

Some become adventures.

And once in a very long while, someone begins to feel like home.
