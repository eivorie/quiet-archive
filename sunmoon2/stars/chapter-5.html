Chapter V

Beneath the Surface

Twelfth House Overlays Moon · Mercury · Venus

Some doors are never opened.

They simply are not meant to be.

And yet, every now and then, someone walks into a room you never realized existed.

Astrology calls it the Twelfth House.

The place beneath language.

Beneath certainty.

Beneath everything we consciously choose to show the world.

It is where forgotten fears sleep.

Where quiet tenderness waits.

Where feelings often arrive before explanations do.

Somehow, our charts keep meeting there.

My Moon wanders through your hidden sky.

Your words find places inside me I hadn't learned to name.

And your Venus...

It doesn't knock.

It simply finds the forgotten window and quietly lets itself in.

Some people are understood.

Some people are simply felt.

Perhaps that's why certain conversations stay with us long after they've ended.

Why silence sometimes says more than explanations.

Why distance occasionally feels strangely irrelevant.

The Twelfth House is not easy.

It asks us to trust what cannot always be proven.

To stop asking why something is felt...

and simply admit that it is.
