Chapter VI

The Sky Chose to Wait

There are people we could have met a thousand times...

and never truly seen.

Timing is its own constellation.

Perhaps the stars never promised that we would meet.

Perhaps they only promised that if we did, we would recognize something we had never known we were looking for.

Every harmony, every shared rhythm, every quiet understanding written across our charts remained only possibility.

Possibility until one ordinary day two people answered the same message.

The stars drew the map.

We were the ones who decided to walk it.

That is the part astrology can never predict.

Kindness.

Patience.

The decision to stay.

The decision to trust.

The decision to love someone little by little, until one day there is no longer a beginning to point at.

Maybe destiny is not a destination.

Maybe it is simply what happens when two people keep choosing the same direction.

So perhaps this was never about planets.

Or houses.

Or aspects.

Perhaps they were only the sky's quiet way of whispering—

"When the time comes...

you'll know."
