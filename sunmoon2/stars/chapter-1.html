Chapter I

Two Skies

Once, there were only two skies.

They had never met. They knew nothing of one another. They rose and disappeared, each following its own quiet rhythm, unaware that somewhere beyond the horizon, another sky was doing exactly the same.

Distance has always been easy to measure. Time too. But there are things neither distance nor time seem able to explain.

Sometimes, two lives begin to move toward each other long before the people living them ever notice.

We spend years believing that every encounter begins on the day it happens. Yet perhaps some stories have already started, written somewhere above us, long before either of us learned how to look up.

This is not a story about certainty. Nor about destiny.

It is simply the story the sky chose to tell when your stars met mine.
