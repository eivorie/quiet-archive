Chapter III

Becoming

Sun ☉ in the First House

Some people enter our lives.

Others enter the way we see ourselves.

The First House is the place of identity. It is the face we show the world, but also the quieter question hidden beneath it:

"Who am I becoming?"

In our charts, your Sun rises there.

Astrology often describes this placement as one person illuminating the other's sense of self.

It isn't about changing someone into someone else.

It's about making it easier to become more fully who they already were.

Some people make us feel admired.

Some make us feel understood.

Very few simply make us feel more ourselves.

That is the language of the First House.

And perhaps that is why recognition felt so immediate.

Not because someone completed what was missing.

But because someone quietly reflected something that had always been there, waiting to be seen.

Our charts reveal something else, too.

While your Sun rises in my First House, my Venus rises in yours.

Identity answers affection. Affection answers identity.

It is a symmetry that appears only a few times in a lifetime.
