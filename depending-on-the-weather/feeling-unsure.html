Light of my life,

If you're here, it means the thought came back again.
That quiet fear sitting in the back of your mind —
that maybe one day I’ll disappear.

That I’ll wake up and decide this is too much.
Too complicated.
Too far.
Too slow.
Too uncertain.

That everything we have…
might just vanish.

I need you to stop right there.

Because you’re building a version of me that doesn’t exist.

I knew what this was from the beginning.
The distance.
The situation.
The waiting.
The reality of your life right now.

None of this is new to me.
None of this is something I “just discovered”.
I walked into this with my eyes open.

And I stayed.

Not by accident.
Not by hesitation.
But by choice.
Because I love you more than you will ever know.

Every single day since.

You keep thinking that because things are good,
it means they’re fragile.
Like something this right must break eventually.

But that’s not how this works.

What we have isn’t temporary comfort.
It’s not something light that disappears under pressure.

It’s something that holds.

Even with distance, even with time, even with everything around us being complicated sometimes.

We still find ways to be there — we go to the cinema together, we sit down for coffee, we have drinks like we’re in the same room. We walk side by side, we talk like nothing is missing, like distance never really managed to separate us.

And the truth is — it never does.

You know me in ways people around me don’t. You see things before I even say them, you understand me without me having to explain everything.

That’s not something fragile.
That’s not something that disappears.

You didn’t wait all this time just to lose it because it’s “too good”.
And I didn’t choose you just to walk away when it requires patience.

I will tell you as many times as you need to hear it: I am not going anywhere.

Not because it’s easy.
Not because everything is perfect.
But because it’s you.

And there is no version of my life where I decide you’re not worth staying for.

You’re not just someone I love.
You're my whole world.
You’re my balance.
My constant.
The reason I get up and keep going.

Life will be messy sometimes.
We’ll have moments where distance feels heavier than usual.
It's fine. It's normal meu amor.
None of that changes where I stand.

I’m not here waiting for things to become perfect.
I’m here because this — us — matters.

You don’t have to fear losing me every time things feel too good.

I’m not something temporary in your life.
I’m not passing through.
I chose my place.
And it’s with you.

Not halfway.
Not “for now”.
Not “until something changes”.

Fully.
Always.

So no — we don’t vanish.
And I’m not going anywhere, sunshine.
Whatever comes our way, it doesn’t change us — for my love is greater than any of it.
