When my words fail my heart

If you're reading this, it probably means your mind convinced you once again that I'm slowly learning how to live without you.

That I'm becoming comfortable with your absence.
That every goodbye hurts me a little less.
That maybe one day... it won't hurt at all.

If that's where your thoughts brought you,
then let me answer them instead of letting them answer for me.

We don't love differently.

We love with the same certainty. The same devotion. The same willingness to build a life around each other.
What differs... isn't the love.

It's the road it takes before it reaches the surface.

Your heart speaks first.
Mine thinks first.

Yours arrives like a wave.
Mine settles like the tide.

But they both reach the same shore.

You feel before you speak. I often think before I realise my heart had already answered.

That difference has never meant I love you less. It only means my love doesn't always use the same language yours does.

Sometimes I choose words that make sense instead of words that feel right.
And I know... sometimes those words hurt you.

They make you wonder whether something changed. Whether I've become more distant. Whether I'm preparing myself for a life that doesn't include you.

But what if I told you... my heart never moved at all?

Do you know why I tell you to go back to sleep? Not because I don't want another hour with you. Because I know you'll sacrifice your own rest if I let you. Because I know how tired you are after another double shift.

Because loving you sometimes means protecting you from the version of yourself that would always choose me before your own body.

If I tell you to sleep, it's because I love you enough to miss you for another hour.

That's not relief.

That's care.

You told me you worry I'm getting used to your absence.

Meu amor... I don't think you realise how present you are in every hour you're physically absent.

You're there when I drive home. You're there when I hear a song that sounds like you. You're there every time my phone lights up. You're there when it doesn't.

You're there while I'm working. You're there when I wonder whether you've eaten. You're there when I count the hours before you'll wake up.

You're there when I cook. You're there in the pictures I take. You're there in the places I suddenly want to visit. You're there in the little walks I would've never taken before.

Without even noticing, you've become part of the choices I make every single day. You've made me want to live my days instead of simply letting them pass.

That isn't absence. That's love finding a thousand places to exist.

You think my silence means I'm okay without you. But silence has never meant emptiness with us.

We send each other good mornings even while the other sleeps. We tell each other when we've arrived safely. We don't do those things because we have to. We do them because somewhere along the way, your life quietly became part of mine.

Checking on you isn't a routine.

It's home.

Every future I allow myself to imagine... has you in it.

Not because I'm trying to comfort you. Because that's genuinely how I picture my life.

You once told me you don't call me your soulmate to sound romantic. You say it because that's simply the truth your heart lives in.

I understand that. Perfectly.

Because it's the same truth mine lives in.

I don't see you as one possibility among others.

So if your mind ever whispers that I'm becoming happy without you...

Remember the life we're already building.

Somewhere without even noticing...
I stopped imagining my life.
I started imagining ours.

That's why I count the hours until you wake up.

That's why every notification makes me hope it's you.

That's why an empty passenger seat feels wrong.

That's why my first instinct is always, "I can't wait to tell him."

You are not someone I occasionally miss.
You have quietly become part of how I experience every single day.

If you ever find yourself wondering where my heart stands...
Remember this.
It has never, not once,
spoken a name other than yours.
