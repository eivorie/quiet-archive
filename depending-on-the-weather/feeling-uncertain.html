It takes two. ♥

If you’re here, it means something feels off between us.
Not broken — but shaken.
And I know you’ve been scared.

Scared I might leave.
Scared something changed in the way I see you.
Scared that maybe this is the kind of thing we don’t come back from.

I get it.
I really do.
But I need you to hear this clearly:
I’m still here.

There is no version of me that ever leaves you.

What we have doesn’t disappear because of one fight.
It doesn't weaken because things got uncomfortable.
If anything, it shows that what we have is real enough to be challenged.

I’m not perfect with this.
I don’t process everything instantly.
Some things stay in my head longer than they should.
I struggle sometimes — you know that.

But it’s not just me.
You feel things deeply too.
you react, you overthink, you get scared in your own way.

And that’s okay.
None of this is one-sided.
We’re both in it.
We’re both learning each other, sometimes gently, sometimes the hard way.

What matters isn’t that we feel things.
What matters is what we choose to do with them.

And I choose you.
Not only when it’s easy.
Not only when everything feels perfect.
I choose you when it’s messy too.
When it’s uncomfortable.
When it takes effort.

I will always come back to you.
To talk.
To understand.
To fix what needs to be fixed instead of walking away from it.

Because we deserve this.
We deserve to love each other freely,
without constantly fearing that everything will fall apart.

Distance makes everything harder right now.
It amplifies doubts, stretches silence, twists small things into bigger ones.
But it doesn’t define us, it's not stronger than what we are.

One day, this won’t just be words.
You’ll see and feel it in how I stay,
how I show up,
how I choose you without hesitation.
Constantly.
Everyday.

For now, this is what I can give you:
I'm here.
I’m not leaving.
I’m not giving up.
And nothing is taking me away from you.
You matter so much more than I know how to put into words.

I love you.
 Not temporarily.
Not conditionally.
But in this sick way only us understand.
