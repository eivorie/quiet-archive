It never fades...

If you're here, my beautiful sunshine, it means something inside you needed to feel held — needed to hear, again, that you are safe with me. So stay. Let me remind you.

My love for you doesn’t flicker. It doesn’t shrink when you worry, or dim when you doubt. It doesn’t disappear when you’re tired, or jealous, or quiet, or when you think you’re “too much.” I’m here — in all the versions of you.

You are the greatest thing that ever happened to me. You bring light into my days in ways you don’t even notice. The way you laugh, the way you tell stories, the way your voice warms when you’re sleepy or soft — all of it brings me peace. You give me comfort just by existing.

I love the little things you do without thinking: the way you get needy when you miss me, the tiny pout when I don't wake you with a vocal text, the way you ask for more like you’re not sure you’re allowed to. You are. You always are. I love you like that — openly, endlessly, without hesitation.

And I don’t just love the sweet parts. I love your fire, the intensity in you, the way you feel everything with your whole heart. I love your devotion, your protective nature, the passion you carry in everything you do. You are complex, beautiful, real — and completely mine.

I love your mind: the way you think, react, argue, dream, worry, hope. And I love your body: the expressions you make, the softness you hide, every detail you don’t even notice about yourself. There isn’t a single part of you that I don’t adore.

And meu amor… there is something else you need to know. I don’t just love who you are — I crave the life I imagine with you every day.

I think about walking around with your hand in mine, about being one of those annoying couples who share a coffee, sitting too close, laughing at nothing, not caring how we look because we’re too busy looking at each other.

I imagine waiting for you to come home after work, hearing the door, crossing the room just to kiss and hug you while you tell me everything about your day — the good parts, the stressful parts, the little stories you love to share that I love to hear. And I imagine you doing the same with me. A life built on small routines that mean everything.

I picture sending you a text in the middle of the afternoon saying, “Let’s go on a date tonight,” and feeling that spark in my chest knowing that you’d smile at your phone before saying yes.

I think about you constantly — in the quiet rhythm of my days. You’re a presence in everything I do. Always in my thoughts. Always in my heart. You exist in me like something permanent.

So if today your heart feels a little heavier — if you’re afraid of losing me, if you wonder whether you’re enough, if your doubts are louder than your certainty — come closer. Let me tell you what is true:

My love doesn’t fade. Not overnight. Not after a fight. Not because you’re scared or unsure. You are loved — fiercely, fully, constantly — in every version of yourself.

I choose you, meu amor. Every day, every mood, every moment. I’m not going anywhere. You are loved. Fiercely. Fully. Constantly. At your highest, at your lowest, and in every quiet place in between. And nothing about that changes — not today, not tomorrow, not ever.
