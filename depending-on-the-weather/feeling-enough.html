When you forget how gentle you are

If you’re here, amor da minha vida, maybe today is one of those days where you feel like you take too much space or ask too much of me. So let me tell you softly — you don’t. Allow me to show you why.

Sometimes you doubt your place with me, as if you somehow slipped into my life without meaning to or stayed longer than you were allowed. But nothing about you has ever felt accidental. You didn’t push your way in — you fitted right away. Instantly, naturally, the way something right finds its space without noise or effort.

I never had to make room for you. My days simply rearranged themselves around you as if they had been waiting for that shape all along. There is no version of my routine, my quiet moments, my tomorrow where you feel out of place. You’re not something extra I carry — you’re a part of what makes everything feel complete.

And then there are the days when you worry you take up too much of me, as if wanting me or being close could ever be a burden. But the truth is simple: the space you occupy is space I want you in.

I know I’m someone who needs silence, stillness, distance — just like you do. But that part of me has never reacted to you the way it reacts to the world. People drain me. Crowds, noise, expectations — all of it pulls at me. But you don’t. You’re the one place where nothing in me feels stretched thin.

Time with you doesn’t wear me out; it gives me back pieces of myself. There’s a steadiness you bring — a warmth that settles instead of taking. The world fades out when I'm granted any second with you. The quiet I find with you isn’t the kind I search for when I’m tired; it’s the kind that makes me feel alive.

And on the days you look at yourself and think you’re “too much,” I wish you could feel what I feel. You’ve never overwhelmed me — not your emotions, not your presence, not the way your heart reaches for mine.

You’re not a weight, not a demand, not something I need to recover from. You’re the soft pull in my chest when everything else gets loud, the calm that settles in places I didn’t even know were restless. You don’t take too much space in my life — you make space inside me I didn’t have before.

Sometimes you also worry that asking for care makes you demanding, like you’re crossing some invisible line or leaning too heavily on me. But loving you has never felt like something that requires strength to hold. Taking care of you doesn’t tire me — it settles me. It reminds me that connection can be soft, that affection can be steady, that closeness can feel like coming home instead of giving something up.

I don’t run out of patience with you. I don’t run out of affection. There isn’t a version of us where you ask for too much because nothing you’ve ever needed has felt like a burden to me. Your presence doesn’t press on me — it roots me. Your closeness doesn’t suffocate — it makes everything in me loosen and breathe. Being there for you doesn’t empty anything in me; it fills places I didn’t know were hollow.

And there is something else, something I don’t always know how to say: I owe you a lot. More than you think. You’ve given me comfort, gentleness, trust — pieces of yourself you didn’t have to share, things I treasure more than I know how to express. And if I’m ever afraid of anything, it’s not you, it’s the idea of not giving you back everything you deserve. Because you deserve so much, meu amor. And I want to rise to that — every day, in every way I can.

Then, there are the moments when you forget how gentle you truly are. You think of yourself as intense, or messy, or overwhelming — but I wish you could see how your tenderness appears without you even noticing it. In the way your voice softens when you’re tired, in the way you worry for me so naturally, in the way you linger, not out of neediness, but out of care.

There’s a softness in you that doesn’t announce itself, but it’s there — in the way you speak, in the way you listen, in the way you hold space for me without realizing you’re doing it. You call yourself a lot of things, but you rarely call yourself gentle, even though it’s one of the first things I feel from you.

So if today you forgot your place in my life — if you doubted your worth, your gentleness, your right to ask for care — let this bring you back to yourself. You have never been too much for me. Not in your presence, not in your emotions, not in the way you love. You are exactly enough. More than enough. And whether you feel steady or shaken, you are the calmest place my heart knows. I love you with everything I have, sunshine.
