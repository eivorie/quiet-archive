Let's breathe ~

Before anything else, before the breathing and the grounding and the words — remember our code, meu amor.

If something feels wrong and you don’t have the energy to explain it, if the world is spinning too fast or too tight inside your chest, the black flag is enough. One tap. One sign.
It means: call me.
It means: I need you now.

You never need to hesitate. You never need to worry about the moment, the hour, the context. That signal exists so you don’t have to struggle alone. And I hope — truly — that when things get heavy, you’ll reach for me before you reach for silence.

But just in case you’re here because you didn’t have the strength to call, or because you didn’t want to disturb me, or because you tried to handle everything alone — then let this page hold you for a moment. Let these words say what I would have said on the phone: that I love you, deeply and without pause. And that you are never, never a burden.

If you're here, amor da minha vida, it means something inside you feels tight again — a weight you didn’t choose, a pressure you didn’t ask for, but that still found its way into your chest.

I won’t tell you what you’re feeling. Only you know that. But if the world suddenly feels too loud, or too quick, or too heavy for your breath to keep up, I want you to know this: you don’t have to go through it alone.

Whether your body aches, whether your thoughts won’t settle, whether your heart tumbles over itself for reasons you can’t explain — I am here. For you. Without hesitation.

If I’m asleep, you can wake me. If we’re far, you can call me. If we argued earlier, it doesn’t matter. Your well-being is always bigger than any silence between us.

Sit with me for a moment. Breathe a little slower. Let your shoulders drop, even just a fraction. Let your heartbeat find something steady to follow.

You don’t need to force calm. You don’t need to “fix” whatever this is. You just need to give yourself permission to pause, to let the storm inside you soften at its own pace.

And if your thoughts are too loud, try not to follow them. Let them pass like distant noise — not doors you must open, not roads you must walk.

Think, if you can, of something small that warms you: a memory, a place, a scent, a moment. Or let your mind stay blank. Both are allowed. Both are enough.

If your body is tense, try placing one hand over your chest and the other on your stomach — not to control anything, just to feel that you’re still here, still breathing, still moving through the moment even if it feels heavy.

And if your lip hurts, love, if it’s irritated again from worry or nerves, please be gentle with yourself. That little mark isn’t a flaw — it's your body asking for softness, not punishment.

You don’t need to be strong right now. You don’t need to pretend you’re fine. You don’t need to carry this alone.

And if everything feels overwhelming, remember you can always step out of the moment for a minute: grab a glass of cold water, feel the floor under your feet, listen to the quiet around you, anything that reminds your body it is not trapped.

I’m here, sunshine. For your fear, your breath, your trembling, your silence. For every anxious hour that asks too much of you. You can lean on me — anytime, in any state. You’re safe with me.
