It feels different — lighter in a way I can’t quite explain.
It’s without a doubt because you were simply there again. Not beside me, not in the room, but present enough that my whole day loosened.

It still surprises me how much your presence changes the way I breathe.
I wake up differently when I know you’re with me — calmer, steadier, like the air isn’t as heavy as it usually is.

And it’s strange, isn’t it? How something so simple — a message from you, your voice, even just knowing you’re awake somewhere — can make everything feel more manageable.

I think I forget, sometimes, how intense my own feelings can get. How quickly they flare, how deeply they run. But when it comes to you, the softer things grow just as fast as the sharper ones. The love grows the quickest. I can calm myself when I’m angry, soften the edge of my jealousy, quiet the panic when it tries to rise… at least a little. But the love? That one never slows down. It never pauses, never fades into the background, never waits its turn. It’s the wild part of me — the one I can’t temper, the one that stays no matter what mood I’m in.

Hearing your voice today… it made me smile without even meaning to.
You don’t even try, and still something in me settles. I listen and all I can think is, that’s mine, and the whole world goes quiet for a moment.

Yesterday you thought I might have taken down your letter from my wall. Sunshine... never. My love for you doesn’t disappear because of fear or anxiety, no matter how strong they feel in the moment. Loving you isn’t something I switch on and off — it isn’t optional, it isn’t fragile. It just is. Always.

I love you in a way that feels warm — like sunlight through a window, like something that finds me gently.
A kind of love that makes me want to lean in, not run from it.

And I want to give you everything I can. Because wanting you — choosing you — feels like the most natural instinct I have.

So yes… today felt lighter. And maybe that’s all it is: the simple relief of knowing you’re with me, and the quiet certainty that I love you — more and more, without even trying.
