2025.12.29

I’m angry.
That’s really an understatement.
I can’t stand imagining that you were ever with someone else.
I can’t stand knowing your past experiences meant something to you.
I know it’s ridiculous, because neither you nor I are the type who “date just for fun.”
But I can’t stand it.
Thinking that you’ve always done everything with passion — with someone else.

I can’t stand everything we talked about before,
the stories, the details you gave.
That stuff never disappears, you know.
It just waits.
One insecurity, one doubt, and it comes right back up.

I know I trust you.
But I also know I hate that entire part of your life.
I hate that you were able to be so committed to someone,
no matter the reason behind it.
And when I reread old messages, I see that you never hid it —
but somehow I chose not to see it,
or ignored it,
or pretended it didn’t matter.

I hate that you can even imagine the same thing about me —
but saying that is hypocritical.
You know absolutely everything,
and at the same time sometimes I feel like you know nothing at all.
Because “nothing” is exactly what defines my life before you.
“Nothing” is what I ever got in return.
“Nothing” describes the empty little relations I had.
And yet you think I miss it,
or that I take pleasure in thinking about it.
I don’t.
I never do.
And still I feel like you’ll never believe me either.
This is what hurts the most.

It’s complicated to talk about.
It’s a minefield, even if we always end up having the conversation.
Neither of us wants to hear these things,
but somehow we still need to know them.
We have a problem, don’t we?
We must enjoy hurting like this, in the end.

I barely slept.
I woke up every hour, if not more.
We said we needed to breathe.
You’re working, probably busy.
I said I would sleep, but who am I kidding?
I’m so angry.
And at the same time, every time I wake up, I hope you said something to me.
It’s ridiculous — you don’t want to talk, you made that very clear.
Ironically, I don’t want to talk either.
Right now it would be a mistake; I’m filled with too many bad feelings.
But I hate even more feeling this far away from you.

I want to write to you, right now.
Just so you know I’m thinking about you.
Because I keep worrying that you’ll wake up this morning and think,
“there it is, she forgot about me.”
No. You’re not forgotten.
You’re never in the background.
But I don’t know — I hurt.
I think I’m going to write anyway.
Because no matter how I feel, you are my priority, always.

You must be thinking I’m fine, finally.
That I’m sleeping well.
That I’m enjoying my "free time" without you.
That I'm "in peace".
And as always, you’re completely wrong.
I keep thinking these stories from long before —
stories long dead and buried —
are going to end up pushing you away.
That I disgust you.
That you don’t trust me enough to believe what I say.
Even though I’ve always made it a point — even with the painful things —
to tell only the truth.

I’m not here with you to joke around.
What’s happening here is real.
Every word matters.
Every emotion matters.
Every fight makes me question myself.
Every joy makes me certain that it’s you.

Believe me:
if I had to start over,
I would wait for you.
For me, you are the only love I’ve ever had.

I miss you.
I hate being this far from you.
I had dreams last night,
images I’d rather erase —
situations I never want to see exist in real life.
I can’t stand being like this with you.

My anger is fading;
it’s turning into emptiness now.
I miss you.
I hated this night.
I hate this morning.
I know I’m going to hate this whole day.
But you need it.
So we deal with it.

No matter how hard it is,
no matter if we’re upset,
my love for you is still my number one priority.

I love you.
Don’t forget that.
