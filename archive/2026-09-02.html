I think the most frustrating part is that I still can't name the problem.

I keep turning it around in my head, hoping that eventually I'll land on something that makes sense.

"That's it."

But I never do. I know I feel jealous. Uncomfortably so.

And I also know something that isn't particularly pretty to admit. Part of me wishes there had never been happy memories before me. I know how awful that sounds. It doesn't make it any less true.

Ereri has always felt like something that belonged to us. Something untouched. Entirely ours.

And knowing someone else once occupied a corner of that world... I hate how much it bothers me.

He tells me it wasn't the same. That if he'd known I existed, things would have been different.

I believe him. That's never really been the problem. The problem is that it still happened.

Sometimes I scroll through his profile and I can barely keep looking.

She's there.

In old pictures. In highlights. In places they've been. In photographs she took.

It's strange. I don't even know if I can still call it jealousy anymore. It feels closer to an unhealthy obsession. What scares me even more is that I always feel the need to bring it back into our conversations.

Because... what exactly am I expecting? The past won't disappear because I keep talking about it. Neither of us has that kind of power.

If I could rewrite history, mine certainly wouldn't stay untouched either.

Lately I've also been wondering whether I've become a little too comfortable. Comfortable enough that I say everything the moment I feel it. Sometimes I wonder if I'm simply failing to show restraint. I've never really had to teach myself when to stay quiet, usually my problem is not keeping everything to myself.

Somehow, with him, the opposite happened, and I'm beginning to think that not every emotion deserves to become a conversation immediately. Especially when I already know that all I'm really doing is hurting someone I love.

That's probably the hardest part.

I can't stand the thought that I might be making him question his place beside me. We're not talking about changing a bad habit. We're talking about someone wondering whether they're still enough.

And I hate that.

Because he is.

He always has been.

It usually takes me a little distance before I know how I actually wanted to say something.

Once the emotions settle the words become kinder, because I've had a few minutes to think instead of spiraling.

Honesty has always been the one quality I've been proud of. Maybe the only one, if you ask me. So when he doubts my sincerity... it hurts more than I know how to explain.

He's different from everyone else I've ever known. I accepted that a long time ago. Nothing I learned really applies to him.

And I don't regret that.

Loving him has never felt like an effort.

I don't get tired of trying.

I don't lose patience.

Every difficult conversation leaves me wanting to understand him better, not to love him less.

But I do want to become better at how I handle those moments, less driven by whatever I'm feeling in that instant.

More willing to stop. Think. Breathe. And answer the actual problem instead of my emotions.

This month marks one year.

One year since our first "I love you."

I hope that, more than anything else, he feels loved.

Every day.

Through every disagreement. Every insecurity. Every difficult conversation.

I hope he never doubts that I'll always keep choosing him.

Because I will.
