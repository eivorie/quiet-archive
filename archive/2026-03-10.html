March 10.
You’re 31 today, amor da minha vida.
I still can’t believe I get to write that —
that I get to be the person who says it.

I keep thinking about last year,—
when I wished you a happy birthday without knowing
what you would eventually become in my life.
I remember writing “a very happy birthday to you,”
and you answering, “thanks so much baby ♥ how have you been ?”
We had no idea then. None.
And now here we are — loving each other in ways
neither of us could’ve predicted.

Every year people make wishes for their birthday,
but today I think I’m the one overflowing with them —
wishes for you,
for us,
for everything you haven’t even imagined yet.

I keep thinking about how lucky I am, too,
that your existence overlaps with mine at all.
You came into my life quietly,
and somehow you became the loudest,
warmest,
brightest part of it.
You make everything make more sense without even trying.
You don’t realize how rare that is —
how rare you are.

So today, on your 31st birthday,
I hope the world treats you the way you deserve.
And because it won’t always do that on its own,
here are all the wishes I’m making for you:

I wish you a year where your heart feels light more often than heavy.

I wish you moments that make you laugh the way only you can.

I wish you peace, the deep kind — the kind that reaches the bones.

I wish you courage for the things you want, and softness for the things you fear.

I wish you confidence in the brilliance you refuse to acknowledge.

I wish you surprises that turn into memories.

I wish you mornings that start gently, and nights that end warm.

I wish you the certainty that you’re loved — endlessly.

I wish you joy — enough of it that you stop pretending you don’t deserve it.

I wish you the home you’ve always looked for, inside yourself and beside someone else (better be me, though).

I wish you rest — the real kind.

I wish you clarity when life gets blurry.

I wish you victories that feel earned, not lucky.

I wish you tenderness.

I wish you calm.

I wish you the universe, truly — because nothing smaller would fit you.

And selfishly,
I wish for every other year where I get to be part of your life.
Where I get to hear your voice in the morning,
your thoughts during the day,
your softness at night.
Where I get to love you the way you make love feel real and possible.

You’re 31 today.
You’re my favorite person on the planet.
And you have no idea — absolutely none —
how deeply you’re loved.

Happy birthday, meu amor.
I hope life gives you everything.
And I hope I get to give you the rest.
