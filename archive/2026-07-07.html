I ran.

Not very far. Just far enough to keep myself from saying something I'd regret. Again.

I felt it coming.

That familiar feeling where my thoughts start moving faster than I can catch them. Where every sentence becomes ten more inside my head before I even have the chance to answer.

So I left. Even if I know my peace lies with him, even if I need him more than anything else, there was no way I could make him feel like he'd failed me one more time. He didn't. I did.

Because talking doesn't help.

Every time I try to explain what I'm feeling, it somehow gets bigger. Messier. It hurts him, it hurts me, and somehow we both end up carrying things that weren't there before we started talking.

The strange thing is... I know he hasn't done anything wrong. I know that. That's what makes it so exhausting in my head.

I can't even explain what's happening inside my own head anymore. And if I can't explain it to myself, how am I supposed to explain it to the person I love the most?

I don't want to talk.

I've realized something about myself over the years.

I've always functioned better when nobody asks me what's wrong. When nobody asks me to open every drawer inside my head and sort through everything that's inside.

Because the truth is... I don't know.

I never really stop to ask myself how I'm doing.

If I started questioning every feeling, every fear, every desire, every part of my life that's changing... I honestly think I'd lose my mind.

So I don't. I just keep going. Maybe that's how I ended up here in the first place.

I've spent most of my life with everything neatly locked away.

People have always thought I was calm. Hard to shake. Hard to read. Cold reliability. Emotionless, maybe.

They weren't entirely wrong. And I never cared what people made of it. Feeling something never meant I had to show it. Or share it with the world. Hell... those feelings were mine.

Crying never came naturally to me. Neither did letting people see when something was wrong. Everything stayed behind walls that even I rarely questioned.

Somehow... those walls don't work with him.
I don't know when they disappeared. I don't know if they were there to begin with.

What amazes me the most is that it wasn't something that happened over months.

It was there from the very beginning.

Every time he asked me something, I wanted him to know the answer. Every single one. I wanted him to know who I was before I even understood why it mattered so much.

I never wanted to keep pieces of myself away from him. I wanted him to have all of them.

Every memory.
Every fear.
Every stupid thought.
Every part of me.

Take it all. Live inside me. Know me so completely that there would never be a place where you didn't belong.

Devour all of it.

I only know that somewhere along the way I forgot how to hide what I feel whenever he's involved. And for a moment, that scared me. Especially a year ago.

I cry because I miss him. I cry because I'm frustrated. I cry because I'm angry. I cry because I can't make my own mind be quiet.

For someone who's spent years feeling almost nothing on the outside... it's strange that the happiest I've ever been is also the most emotional I've ever become.

Maybe that's what happens when someone finally reaches the parts of you that nobody else ever could.

Yet somehow, beneath all that noise, I still know there has never been anything equal to us.

Maybe ignoring things isn't healthy. But right now... digging only makes the hole deeper.

I want silence again.

I want to stop giving these thoughts more room than they deserve. They're slowly moving into our everyday life, and I refuse to let them live there.

Earlier today I tried listening to one of his voice messages.

One I usually love.

He was describing one of those little fantasies of his. A bathroom. Whispering in my ear because we couldn't be too loud. The kind of message that usually makes me smile before I even finish listening.

But my mind betrayed me.

It took his words and replaced me.

It replayed something he'd once told me about his past instead.

Suddenly it wasn't us anymore.

I couldn't keep listening.

I closed it.

That's when I realized how much space these thoughts have started taking.

They're stealing moments that were never supposed to belong to them.

I don't know why. I know it isn't rational. I know it isn't fair. But knowing doesn't make the pictures disappear.

I can't bear imagining that someone else once held the person I love.

I can't make those images stop.

And every explanation only gives them more details.

So I don't want to explain anymore.

I don't want to ask.

I don't want to know.

I want to forget that I ever learned how to imagine those things.

I forgot how to ignore them.

I need to remember.

Maybe silence isn't running away.
Maybe, just for now, it's the kindest thing I can give both of us.

I don't need answers anymore.
I just want to find my way back to the quiet, and to him, without anything blocking my way.
