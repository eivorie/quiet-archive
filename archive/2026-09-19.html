One year.

One year since "I am in love with you."

To celebrate, we each booked a room in our own city.

He had French food.

I had Brazilian.

We wanted to share a meal together, even if an ocean still stood between us.

Afterwards, we went out for drinks.

It went a little better for me than it did for my poor amorzinho.

We stayed on the phone almost the entire time.

Hours and hours.

We kept sending each other pictures.

Our food.

The places we were sitting.

The drinks we ordered.

His outfit.

God...

He's so beautiful.

To think that out of everyone, he chose me.

I still can't believe it.

I catch myself staring at his picture wondering how I got this lucky.

I'll never get used to looking at him.

Falling asleep to the sound of his voice...

Waking up to him asking,

"Amor... are you sleeping?"

Wonderful.

I could listen to his voice forever. His voice that makes everywhere feel like home.

I don't even care what he's talking about.

He could read me a grocery list, and I'd still melt. How unfair.

Sharing that kind of intimacy over a phone call...

A year ago, I wouldn't even have imagined it.

Today, it felt perfectly natural.

Wonderful.

I wonder what our future has in store for us.

More than that, I wonder how much courage it will ask of me.

Because he is a world of his own.

And I found home there.

I try to imagine what it'll be like.

Feeling his skin under my fingertips.

Running my hand through his hair instead of wishing I could.

Finally knowing what he smells like.

Memorizing it.

So I never have to imagine it ever again.

Hearing his voice without speakers, without headphones, without oceans.

I don't think I've ever loved him quietly.

If anything, I've only gotten worse.

More attached.

More protective.

More certain that he's my person.

My soulmate.

I thought love would eventually become familiar.

It never did.

He still makes my heart race exactly the same.

Every "I love you" still feels like the first one.

I laughed so much today.

I also wanted to cry.

We both knew coming back to "ordinary" life would hurt.

It already does.

I miss him.

But today we celebrated one whole year of being completely, hopelessly, mad about each other.

I can't wait to celebrate the next one.

And every one that comes after.

Thank you for taking the day off.

Thank you for staying with me.

Thank you for turning ordinary days into memories I'll keep forever.

Thank you for accepting me exactly as I am.

Thank you for loving me with a passion I will spend my whole life returning.

If you ever wonder whether you're loved enough...

There will never be an "enough."

Because I can never be done loving you more.

And believe me, I already love you more than the world can carry.

Happy first year, meu amor. Here's to all the ones we still have ahead of us.
