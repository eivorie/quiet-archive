They say Friday the 13th brings bad luck, but I started mine in the best possible way — with him. And honestly, that alone rewrites the whole superstition.

Every day with him feels different. In one second I can feel unbearably far from him, missing everything at once, and the very next second — when he’s here, when he answers, when he’s present — it hits me like a wave. That feeling of him filling every corner of my mind, every part of me waking up just because he exists again.

The more intimacy we share — even through messages — the stronger everything gets. It’s like every conversation pulls me closer, makes me feel him deeper, leaves me even more attached after. And once he’s gone again, even for a moment, it’s worse. I feel inhabited by him, and the distance hits harder than before.

Maybe that’s why we say we love each other more every day. Maybe that’s why the smallest shift in his tone can make alarms go off in my head. Because I feel him so intensely that even the idea of him being far terrifies me.

Tomorrow is Valentine’s Day here in France. Not in Brazil — he told me theirs is in June. But honestly, tomorrow isn’t the reason to celebrate anything. I should celebrate him every day. For what he brings into my life isn’t seasonal, it’s constant.

So today he’ll find a small thing — something honest, something short — where I try to put into words what he gives me every single day and what he represents in my life. I know my writing isn’t like his; the poem he gave me for Christmas was beautiful in a way I don’t think I’ll ever be able to match. But even if mine is more modest, I hope he’ll love it just as much — because every line is him.
I want him to know I love him — every day, not just on a date on the calendar.

And when the day comes where I can show that love physically too, he’ll understand it fully — that nothing can stop the tenderness and the devotion I carry for him. Not distance, not time, not anything. Just him.
