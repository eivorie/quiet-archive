I’ve been thinking a lot about the questions I used to ask myself in the beginning —
back when I first started catching feelings for you.
I wondered if I could handle it.
Handle the intensity, the distance, the fear, the parts of you that woke up old wounds in me.
And the truth is… this was never something unexpected.
I knew even then that loving you would expose every fragile place inside me.

But the answer is yes.
Yes, I can deal with it.
Yes, I can deal with all of it —
as long as it means being with you.

I see now that I carry a lot of past traumas I never addressed properly.
Things I had to live once, things that didn’t leave me whole.
And when something matters as much as you do,
those old fractures rush back to the surface.
Not because you caused them,
but because you’re the first person who makes me want to heal them.

And it’s not just about me —
I think about you too, all the time.
About what you carry, about the weight you don’t always show, about the battles you fight quietly. Your part isn’t easy, and I know that. I care about you in ways I don’t always know how to express.
I want the best for you — truly, deeply —
not because I feel obligated, but because loving you means wanting your life to feel lighter. I want to be someone you can rely on, someone who stands with you when things get complicated, someone who doesn’t look away when it gets hard. I don’t want to be just the person you love — I want to be the person who helps you breathe a little easier.

And it’s manageable.
All of it.
I can work through the shadows, through the fears, through the moments when my mind twists things
that have nothing to do with us.
I can learn to ignore the outside noise, the behaviors of others, the ghosts of before.
Doubts will come, because they come to everyone —
but the bigger truth has always been us.
And I’m not ready to let go.

I’m here for every moment — good, bad, confusing, painful, soft.
Loving someone means you don’t get to choose the easy parts and discard the rest.
You show your value by staying, by showing up,
by standing beside your other half even when the road is uneven.
What we have has to be stronger than my old instincts,
stronger than the parts of me that try to act like someone I’m not.

And today… after everything… talking to you felt like breathing again.
Like finally finding the missing piece I misplaced for a day.
Even if the problem isn’t fully solved yet,
even if it takes time to settle,
the moment we talked,
something inside me steadied.

It felt like a blessing —
like coming home after being lost for hours.
And it reminded me of the simplest truth in all of this:
I want this.
I want us.
I love you.
Completely.
