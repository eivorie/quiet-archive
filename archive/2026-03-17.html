It feels strange when he’s not around as much as I’m used to.
Not wrong — just… off.
Like something in the rhythm of my days is missing,
like the world keeps moving but not quite the way it should.

I notice it more than I should.
The silence isn’t the same anymore.
I used to like it.
Now it just feels empty in a way I can’t fix on my own.
Nothing really hits the same until he’s there again.
Until I hear from him.
Until I feel him.

And it’s stupid because I try to act normal about it.
“He’s busy, it’s fine, go do your own thing.”
Yeah, sure.
Doesn’t change the fact that everything feels slightly off without him.

It’s like everything pauses slightly when he’s gone,
and then suddenly —
the moment he comes back —
everything starts living again.
Like the world remembers how it’s supposed to work.
Like I, remember how it's supposed to work.

Last week, at this exact moment, I was having coffee with him for his birthday.
And somehow, it felt like I was the one being gifted.
I had such a good time with him — like I always do —
effortless, natural, exactly where I was supposed to be.

And the weirdest part is that
the more I try to live my life normally here,
the closer I feel to him.
Eating on my own,
walking around,
looking for small places I know he would like,
quiet cafés, warm corners, soft lights —
he’s there in all of it.

I catch myself thinking,
he would love this,
or I wish he was here with me,
and suddenly it doesn’t feel like I’m alone anymore.
It feels like I’m carrying him with me,
everywhere I go.

But that makes it worse too.
Because the closer I feel to him in those moments,
the more I realize he’s not actually here.
And the missing hits harder,
deeper,
sharper than before.

I don’t think I’ll ever get used to that.
To the distance.
To the quiet in between.

I just know that when he’s there,
everything feels right again.
And when he’s not,
I’m just waiting to tell him "I love you" again.
