This morning I woke up earlier again. I always do. Life doesn’t really make it easy for us with six hours of difference between our days, so I take whatever time I can get with you. Anything. Just like you stay awake way too late to have a few extra minutes with me. We never sat down and decided anything — it just happened on its own. Naturally. Quietly. As if our bodies learned the rhythm before we did.

Last night I struggled to even wish you a good day at work. I knew I’d miss you, that part is normal, but something about yesterday felt different. Too heavy. Almost impossible to breathe through. I needed you — not in a dramatic way, not even in a jealous way — just… a pure necessity. Like something inside me would snap if I didn’t have you close. I don’t know how to manage that kind of feeling yet. I don’t know where it comes from or where it goes. It’s new, and it’s tied to the love I have for you, this love I’m still learning but that forever feels irreversible.

I wasn’t looking for an answer to it. I just knew one thing: I missed you. More than yesterday, more than the days before. I just missed you.

So this morning I woke up early, and you were there. Fresh out of the shower, back home, ready to slip into our little routine like nothing had ever gone wrong inside my chest. You made me laugh, and at the same time my heart hurt a little — that soft kind of ache that comes from wanting someone too much. I needed you still, maybe even more than the night before.

And then we ended up loving each other the way we always do — in that way that feels new every time, strong every time, your touch a little familiar every time. There’s still something in it that catches me off guard, something I fully recognize that I trust completely.

I love you without any reason attached to it. I miss you even when you're with me. I can’t go without you — not the way I used to go without people. You're my world, my calm, my heart in its safest place.

And if this is what loving you feels like — I’ll take it. Every sunrise, every distance, every ache. Eternally. Because you're the best part of me.
