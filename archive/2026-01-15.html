I think I’m understanding what being happy feels like.
Not the dramatic version I always pictured — just this soft, easy feeling
that’s been sitting in my chest lately. It’s warm, uncomplicated, and it shows up every time you’re here with me. I didn’t expect happiness to look like this. But I’m glad it does.

Being with you — even in the simplest ways — has become my favorite part of every day.
Watching shows together, sending each other updates, hearing about your day,
waiting for you to wake up or come back from work…
none of it is “special” to the world, but it feels like treasure to me.

I feel good when you’re here.
The kind of good that doesn’t need a reason.
It’s just there — steady, warm, almost grounding.
Like I can breathe a little easier because you exist on the other side of the screen.

And I love loving you.
Not in some dramatic way — just in the everyday sense.
I love having you to share things with, to plan things with,
you — who makes even the waiting feel worth it.

And when we finally get to be together,
I know I won’t always have the perfect words —
talking isn’t exactly my strongest skill.
But my love shows up in small things:
the little gestures, the attention,
the way I want to look after you without making a big deal out of it.
That’s where my heart speaks the loudest.

I keep imagining what we’ll do next,
little plans, trips, moments we haven’t lived yet.
And it makes me feel stable somehow.
Like the distance doesn’t win when we’re looking forward together.

You make life feel better.
Even the ordinary parts.

I hope you know how loved you are,
not in big declarations but in all the tiny ways I think of you.
You’re my favorite part of the day.
Every day.
