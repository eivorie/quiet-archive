I don't really know how to feel today.

Maybe frustrated. Maybe simply sad.

He genuinely thinks I don't love him the way I used to.

With the same intensity. The same certainty.

And somehow... there was something strangely reassuring hidden inside that fear.

Not because I enjoyed seeing him hurt. God, no.

But because he still loves me enough to be afraid.

He worries he's too much when he talks about those fears. Too emotional. Too needy. Too difficult.

He isn't.

He never has been.

If anything... hearing him say those things reminds me that his heart is still reaching for mine just as instinctively as mine reaches for his.

It's strange hearing the person you love describe a version of yourself that doesn't exist.

If he spent a single ordinary day inside my head... He'd probably go insane.

I miss him for what feels like a thousand different reasons every day.

Driving past the airport. Seeing a plane cross the sky. Hearing him say he's going to sleep, even though I hate it. Watching him walk somewhere through his camera. Watching the sun rise, or set.

Looking at a picture of a café. A park. A random street.

Everything immediately turns into, "I wish I were there with him."

I catch myself cooking and wondering if he'd like it.

Taking pictures because I know he'll enjoy them. Or be annoying. But I love it anyway.

Going outside more often because, somehow, he made me want to experience things instead of hiding at home.

Before him... I'd happily spend entire weekends inside.

Now I actually look for little things worth sharing.

He has quietly become the reason I pay more attention to the world around me.

It turns out life is much more interesting when you're constantly looking for things you can't wait to show someone.

Tomorrow is his mum's birthday. I really hope she has a lovely day.

And I hope he'll be able to go home again soon.

Back to his own bed. His own routine. His own life.

He misses it more than he admits.

I'm still jealous. Ridiculously jealous, sometimes.

Somehow... I also know we'll get through it.

I always tell him I feel it in my belly. He knows exactly why.

Maybe that's also why it's so frustrating.

He only hears my words.

He doesn't know that my hand is going to look for his almost constantly.

That I'll steal hugs for absolutely no reason.

That every time he walks through a door, I'll probably kiss him before saying hello.

That I'll keep looking for his eyes from the other side of the room simply because seeing him there will already make everything feel right.

I already know I'm going to hold his face between my hands far more often than he'll expect.

And kiss his forehead. His cheeks. The tip of his nose. His chin.

Popcorn kisses.

One after another until he laughs and tells me to stop.

I probably won't.

I want to sit beside him and discover that peace people keep talking about.

The kind where nothing extraordinary is happening.

Yet everything feels complete simply because he's there.

I don't dream about adventures nearly as much as I dream about ordinary days.

About reaching for his hand without thinking. Falling asleep on the sofa. Resting my head on his shoulder while he complains about football. Walking through a supermarket arguing over what we're having for dinner, or what will be our next Top Chef adventure.

Maybe that's what he doesn't know yet.

Distance forces my love to stay inside words.

But when we remove the distance...

He'll discover that words were never my favourite way of loving him.

I think I'm going to start writing my hour-by-hour diary again.

Mostly because I want proof.

Proof that the version of me he fears... simply doesn't exist.

If he could read everything that crosses my mind during an ordinary day...

Spoiler alert.

It's an unreasonable amount of him.
