Today we're going to talk about... the "fight."

Except it wasn't really a fight.

It was two people slowly drifting apart.

Somehow... that hurts so much more.

It's been fourteen hours since we've spoken.

It feels like weeks.

I slept through thirteen of them.

I was supposed to think. I gave up almost as soon as I tried.

Nothing about this makes sense to me.

I keep replaying everything in my head, hoping I'll suddenly understand.

But I don't.

What keeps coming back to me is the feeling that, if he had to make the same choice again, even knowing how it would end...

He would still make it.

As if everything I begged him to consider simply didn't matter.

I told him where it would lead.

It felt like he didn't care.

And now I'm the one left here, trying to figure out what to do with all of this.

I think what hurt the most was the feeling that, for a moment... someone else's words mattered more than mine.

We're supposed to be two. Just the two of us. Not three.

It's already difficult enough knowing that some things from the past aren't entirely gone.

And then suddenly... she was right there again.

It felt like they were two again.

And I was left on my own. I was watching them revisit a piece of their history. Watching them interact. When the only thing that ever brought me comfort was believing there was nothing left of that anymore.

"She just got home from work, let me ask her."

Meanwhile I was lying in bed, completely falling apart, my eyes burning with tears, because he'd left me there to talk to her instead.

How stupid of me.

He already knows what I think.

He knows how I feel, and he knew exactly why this would hurt me.

But what's left of me if I simply come back?

What happens if he starts believing it's okay to ignore the limits I've tried so hard to explain, because he knows I'll always find my way back?

And the worst part...

He's right.

I would.

I'd go back immediately.

It doesn't feel like living when he's not here.

Maybe that's what happens when you give someone absolutely everything.

Maybe one day he'll grow used to my absence.

Maybe eventually he'll stop trying, while I'm still here, burning through whatever brain cells I have left, trying to understand what I'm supposed to do now.

It's like a blast from the past.

Numb days.

The kind I used to know far too well.

The kind where I kept moving despite knowing I wasn't really going anywhere.

Then I met Ju.

Somehow... I thought those days were behind me.

I hope they still are.
