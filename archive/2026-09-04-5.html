This is hell.

I should be working.

Instead, I'm sitting here, staring at the same room for what feels like the hundredth time today.

I don't even know why I'm writing this.

I shouldn't be.

I shouldn't even be checking the time anymore.

But you always liked these. Knowing things the time they happen.

Right now, I don't even want to talk about myself.

I don't care what I'm doing.

I just want to know where you are.

What you're doing.

What you can see.

Whether you're okay.

I'd trade every word on this page just to know how your day is going.

To hear "I love you" one more time.

So...

I'm sitting in bed.

The room is quiet.

Nothing has changed.

Except you're not here anymore.

I'm going back to sleep.
