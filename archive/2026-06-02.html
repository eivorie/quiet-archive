I haven’t written in a while. Not because nothing was happening, but because I didn’t know where to place myself in all of it. I didn’t know what to think, or what to do with everything that kept piling up in my head. It felt too much, too messy to put into words without breaking it or misunderstanding it myself.

There were arguments. Long silences. Hours without talking that felt heavier than they should have. There were moments where jealousy took over, on both sides, and turned something beautiful into something sharp. There were questions I couldn’t answer, doubts I didn’t want to admit, and that constant feeling of not knowing if I was reacting the right way, or just reacting.

And then… this.

This calm that settled in without warning. The quiet kind, not empty, not distant. The kind that feels like returning somewhere familiar. The kind that makes everything slow down just enough for you to breathe again. Finding him again in that space, feeling him close in the way only we manage to be, even like this. It’s a peace I didn’t expect to feel so strongly after everything.

It’s strange, because nothing around us is simple. His situation isn’t simple. The distance isn’t simple. The waiting isn’t simple. And yet, with him, it stops feeling like something unbearable. It turns into something we can hold, something we can carry together without it breaking us.

I think what destabilizes me the most is how intense everything is. Everything feels stronger, sharper, louder. The love, the fear, the jealousy, the need to protect what we have. I’ve never felt this kind of territorial pull before. The idea of losing him doesn’t just scare me, it feels completely wrong, like something that shouldn’t even be possible.

Loving him the way I do is new to me. Completely. There’s no reference, no past experience I can rely on to understand it better. And maybe that’s why it shakes me sometimes. But it also makes one thing very clear: I want him to feel it. I want him to feel loved in a way that leaves no room for doubt, even on his worst days, even when his mind turns against him.

What we have now, this intimacy we found again, it feels different. Softer, but stronger at the same time. Like something that’s been tested and didn’t collapse. We talk, we share, we exist together in ways that don’t make sense to anyone else. And somehow, it’s enough. More than enough.

Today, I feel good. Truly. Not in a fleeting way, not in a “this will pass” kind of way. I feel steady. Almost invincible in a quiet way, just because we are together. Like whatever comes next is something we’ll face, not something that will take him away from me.

I worry about him. About everything he’s dealing with, everything that’s out of his control right now. That part doesn’t leave me. But it doesn’t change anything either. It doesn’t make me step back. If anything, it anchors me more.

It’s funny, because as I’m writing this, I’m sitting here with that stupid electrostimulation belt on, and I know for a fact he would make fun of me for it. And somehow that thought makes me smile. It breaks the seriousness of everything I’m saying, but in the best way possible. Because that’s us too. Not just intensity, not just depth. Also something light, something real, something that breathes.

I think about the future more clearly now. Not in a rushed way, not in a way that tries to control everything. Just… clearer. And damn, was I needing this.

But the missing part… that’s getting harder. Not easier. I miss him constantly. It’s not something that fades into the background. It’s there all the time, woven into everything I do. And some days, it feels almost unbearable. Like my body doesn’t understand why he’s not physically there.

Today, I feel at peace with him. With us. With everything we’re building, even if it takes time, even if it’s not easy.

Today, I feel like nothing is insurmountable, as long as it’s him and me — because even the mere thought of his smile is enough to ground me.
