Well. Yesterday was interesting.

For starters, something ridiculously insignificant gave my ego a little boost. I won't get into the details, partly because they're irrelevant and partly because I'm not particularly proud of how pleased I was.

But for a moment, I felt a little more confident about my place in his life. A place I've been assured is mine, yet somehow still struggled to picture without feeling like I was competing for it.

I know my confidence shouldn't depend on such insignificant things. But apparently, even my ego appreciates a little encouragement now and then.

It's funny, really. How something so small can make me feel secure for a moment, when my own head is perfectly capable of taking that security away five minutes later.

Which brings me to the rather embarrassing discovery.

I managed to upset myself over something he hadn't even said. Again. He made a perfectly innocent comment, my brain took it somewhere unpleasant, and within seconds I was already upset over an intention I'd invented myself.

The worst part is how convincing it feels when it happens. I don't even notice the moment I stop listening to what he's saying and start reacting to whatever I've decided he meant. He notices, though. Apparently I turn cold almost immediately.

Wonderful.

We talked a little about it. About the things we each carry around, the things that make us react before we've had time to think. I stopped us before we got too far into it. He agreed. There's no point opening every cupboard when neither of us has the energy to deal with what's inside.

Then came the subject of spending a few hours apart.

A few hours. Not days. Not disappearing. Just having some time to ourselves without being attached to our phones.

You'd think that was a perfectly reasonable suggestion but apparently, my brain disagrees.

We already have an ocean and six hours between us. The idea of deliberately adding more distance made me feel like even the little time we have together has become too much for him.

I didn't particularly enjoy that thought.

But I didn't turn it into an argument either. I went to sleep, woke up at two in the morning, and told him how I'd felt. I made an effort to explain myself without putting words in his mouth, and when he answered, I tried to take his explanation for what it was.

It went surprisingly well.

I'm not going to congratulate myself for managing one conversation like a functioning adult, but I'll admit I was pleased.

Oh, I got his voice messages back yesterday, too.

For heaven's sake, that voice.

It's a breath of fresh air. An ice-cold bucket of water thrown straight at me. The breath I didn't even realize I'd been holding, finally escaping my lungs. That sudden release of tension in my shoulders, as if my entire body had been waiting for that familiar sound to remember how to relax.

I don't think he has any idea what his voice does to me.

Then, he told me he needed affection. Turns out, he'd dreamed about us. Just being comfortable together, holding each other, kissing.

And there went whatever composure I'd managed to maintain.

I was lying in bed with tears in my eyes, feeling like my chest was going to burst. All because he wanted to be in my arms. I wish he could be. I wish I could turn towards him instead of looking at a screen. Pull him closer, kiss him properly, feel him settle against me. Nothing complicated. Just the luxury of having him there.

One day, I hope I get to be annoyed by how much space he takes up in bed. Even though I plan to stick on his side no matter where he settles. I'd probably be grumpy about it just for the sake of being annoying. I already know I will never mind him being close.

We ended up being rather less detached than we'd planned.

So much for that.

I don't regret a second of it.

I used to think we were already pretty steady. And we were, in our own way. But I'm starting to realize there might be an even better version of us waiting somewhere down the road.

Same two people, same personalities, same everything. Just a little more aware of our own issues, and capable of dealing with them without making a mess of what we have together. Imagine that.

And that's what makes me almost looking forward to sort through my own bullshit. Not because I enjoy it, obviously, it sucks. But because I can see how much stronger we could become.

I thought we were doing pretty well already. Turns out, we might have been underestimating ourselves.
