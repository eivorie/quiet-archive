Of course.

But this morning, I did.

He even told me he loved me last night.

And still, I cried over the fact that I hadn't been awake to answer him.

How ridiculous is that?

I suppose it isn't really about one missed message. It's about everything those messages used to mean. About being part of each other's days, even with an ocean and six hours between us. About the comfort of knowing that, somewhere in the world, there was a person who wanted to tell me he'd made it home.

I miss being that person for him.

Yesterday was awful.

When my messages stopped being delivered, I convinced myself he had done it deliberately. That he had cut me off. That even the smallest courtesy between us was gone.

I was furious, mad worried, scared. I wondered how he could be so cruel when he knew how worried I was.

And then he wrote.

There was something wrong with his connection.

Just like that, all the anger I'd been holding turned into something else. It felt as though someone had thrown a bucket of ice-cold water straight into my face.

Relief. Shame. Fear. Love. Guilt.

All at once.

I had been so fucking scared something had happened to him. Scared that he was driving, that he wasn't okay, that something had gone terribly wrong and I wouldn't even know. And somehow, that fear had turned into the certainty that he was hurting me on purpose.

Before I can understand why I'm terrified, I'm ashamed. Before I can do anything with the shame, I miss him so much that none of the other feelings seem to matter.

And somewhere in the middle of all that, I keep hurting the person I love.

I've had to defend myself against intentions he assigned to me, against meanings he found in my past that were never mine to begin with. I need to learn when to shut my damn mouth.
