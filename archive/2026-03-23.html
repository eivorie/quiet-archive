Some days, I’m a little scared of myself.
Not in a dramatic way — just… surprised.
Like I don’t fully recognize what I’m feeling anymore.

The jealousy.
The territorial instinct.
That constant, physical need
to keep him close,
to take care of him like he’s the most precious thing on earth.
It’s intense. Way more than I’m used to.

And the closer I feel to him,
the worse it gets.
Which doesn’t even make sense.
It should calm me down —
Yet it does the opposite.

I don’t want him away.
Not even a little.
Because the second he’s not there,
something in me just… drops.
I forget how to breathe properly.
Everything feels off, empty, unfinished.
Life is dull, and doesn't make sense.

The longing is awful sometimes.
Like actually awful.
It hurts. It physically hurts.
And there are moments where I just want to scream
that this is unfair —
the distance, the timing, all of it.

The weirdest part is that this isn’t who I usually am.
I’m not the clingy type.
I’m supposed to be composed, independent, detached if anything.
Or at least that’s what I used to be.

Today, I find myself looking for him everywhere.
Asking for more, constantly.
His time, his voice, his attention — all of it.

I’m always trying to stretch time,
to push the hours back just a little more,
to stay in those moments longer than I’m allowed to.

I breathe him in every second I get,
and somehow it’s never enough.
He’s everywhere — and I still need more.
Always more.
I don’t get tired of him, ever.
I never will.
It's impossible.

And now?
Now I feel everything at once.
Strong, messy, sometimes a little too much.
It clashes with who I thought I was.
But I can’t ignore it either.

I know I have things to learn.
Things to understand better.
Ways to love him that are healthier, calmer,
without losing this intensity but not letting it take over either.
I want to get better for us.
For him. For me.

But one thing doesn’t change.
No matter how overwhelming it gets,
no matter how much it scares me sometimes —

my life is him.
And I’ll learn everything I need to
to love him the way he deserves.
