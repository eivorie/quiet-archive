These days feel… strange.

I feel good. But at the same time, I’m very aware that he’s not here — even though he’s everywhere.

I’m talking about my lucky charm.

I don’t really have control over my emotions lately. Not the kind I usually have.

Everything can be fine, perfectly normal — and then it shifts. Just like that. Because of a thought, a word, a sentence.

Everything feels amplified. Louder. Deeper.

I think it’s because I’m fully aware of how rare this is. Having someone like him in my life. And because of that… like any human being, I’m scared it could stop.

What scares me even more is the idea that by being afraid of losing it, I might be the one to break it.

So I’ve been setting small challenges for myself recently. Every day.

Being more direct when something feels off.
Not letting thoughts grow unchecked.
Taking things at the root.

Either I keep it to myself and explore it honestly — and if it makes no sense, like it often does, I accept that it comes from me.

Or, if I can connect it to something real, I talk about it. Even if I don't fully believe in it. Even just to hear him say I'm being crazy.

I wish I could silence that part of my mind sometimes.

The one that takes something small and turns it into something bigger than it should ever be.

I know it doesn’t make sense. I can see it while it’s happening.

But it’s loud anyway.

And I think that’s the hardest part — not the thoughts themselves, but the fact that I know they’re wrong and still have to sit with them.

The problem is… I talk a lot. About everything.

I know he doesn’t mind. I know he actually wants me to talk, but sometimes I feel like I make things harder for him.

He said something the other day — that maybe others had it easier because I talked less with them.

I know he didn’t mean it like that. I know we were both annoyed. I understand what he meant.

Still… it stayed with me.

Right now he’s sleeping. And I miss him.

It’s constant.
Wanting to be better for him — that’s constant too.

Not just small things like stopping vaping. Bigger than that.

I want to be a good partner for him.

He deserves to be loved unconditionally. To receive the same kind of support he gives to everyone else. To feel safe knowing he can rely on me the same way I rely on him all the time.

Even on my worst days, it takes nothing for him to calm me down. I'm not even sure he's aware.

Just a "hi". Just his voice.

The sound of it. The tone.

He could say anything.

And I feel it — a small shiver, and then instantly… it’s like I’m wrapped in something warm.

It’s comfortable.

It’s safe.

And I see it. All the effort he puts in for me.

The way he always takes me into consideration. Always includes me in his thoughts. Always, always pays attention to how I feel. As if I am something worth the trouble.

Even when I don’t say things clearly. Even when I don’t make it easy.
He still shows up the same way. Consistent. Careful. Present.

And I don’t think he realizes how much that matters.

And the truth is… he never really leaves space for doubt.

If doubt exists, it doesn’t come from him.

It comes from me. From the way I fill in the silence with my own insecurities.

I know he worries about that. About making me feel something he never intended.

But he doesn’t. He doesn’t, because he’s trustworthy. Because he tells me everything. Because he’s always willing to explain things to me, no matter how many times I need to hear it. He never makes me feel like I’m too much for asking.

Somehow, he became my guideline.

Not in a way that limits me — but in a way that helps me find the right direction again when my thoughts start going too far.

I didn’t plan to write about my ass again. But I think he needs to know.

I see him. The way he is.

And to me… he’s perfect like that.

Loving him doesn’t feel like something I have to try to do right. It just happens. Effortlessly.

Ele é o meu mundo inteiro.
