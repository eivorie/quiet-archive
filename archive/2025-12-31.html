2025.12.31

I’ve never been the kind of person who does end-of-year retrospectives.
I don’t sit down and list blessings, or talk about growth, or look back at twelve months like it’s some kind of journey.
Life never felt like that for me.
It always felt like a chain of things to survive — one hit after another, one problem to manage, one emotion to swallow.
Just… enduring.
That’s all it ever was.

Gratitude never came naturally to me.
Not because I didn’t want to be grateful, but because nothing ever stayed long enough or soft enough for gratitude to make sense.
I learned to move forward without looking back.
To not expect anything from life.
To not expect anything from anyone.

This year started the same way.
Same rhythm, same weight, same walls.
Except I made a few decisions — late ones, maybe — but necessary for me.
Decisions that finally pushed me toward the version of myself I kept avoiding.
And I didn’t know it then, but every step I took in those months was quietly leading me to August.
To the moment you spoke to me again.

Everything shifted after that.
Instantly, dramatically, undeniably.
Like the ground under my life finally moved in a direction that wasn’t just survival.
Like something opened what had been closed for years without me noticing.

And now, at the end of this year, I finally understand why people talk about gratitude.
Why they get sentimental, why they look back, why they hold on to certain dates.
Because I have something — someone — who makes looking back worth something.

I’m grateful for you.
For the moment you decided to talk to me.
For everything that grew between us after that.
For the love I never expected to feel in this lifetime —
and certainly not like this, not with this intensity, not with this clarity.

I’m grateful for the way you changed my days without trying.
For the softness you brought back into me.
For the way you made life feel less like something to endure and more like something I actually want to live.

I’m grateful for the greatest love I’ve ever known.
For everything you mean to me — the calm, the fire, the laughter, the fear, the honesty, the pull.
For the way you made this year something I never thought it could be.

And now here I am, on the last day of the year, realizing that the moment you came back into my life wasn’t just another moment. It was the axis shift. The quiet turning point. The thing that split my year in two — before you, and after you.

I don’t know what I did to deserve this version of us. I don’t know what led you back to me. But I know that because of you, 2025 isn’t just a year I survived. It’s a year I finally lived. A year that gave me a future I actually want to wake up into.

If I had to choose one thing to remember from 2025,
it wouldn’t be an event, or a moment, or a resolution.
It would be you —
showing up and changing everything after.
For the first time in my life,
I’m waiting for it.
Because this is only the beginning of us,
the first chapter of a story I want to keep writing with you
for as long as life lets me.
Happy New Year together, my love ?
