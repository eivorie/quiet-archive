Yesterday wasn't our best day.

We didn't really fight. It was one of those conversations where you can almost see the other person's thoughts getting louder than your own voice. We both carry the same kind of retrospective jealousy, just pointed in opposite directions. Yesterday it was his turn to drown in it.

And honestly... I understood every single feeling.

That's the strange thing about it. When you're standing outside of it, everything sounds irrational. But when you're the one inside it, every fear suddenly feels like undeniable proof.

I tried to stay as calm as I could because I could see how much it was hurting him. More than anything, I wanted to make the pain disappear. I wanted him to stop looking at himself through that lens for just a second.

It made me realize something I hadn't truly understood before.

Whenever I'm the jealous one, I always think I'm the only person suffering. My head gets so loud that I forget someone is standing on the other side of those words. Someone who didn't deserve them.
Yesterday I saw that side again.

I saw how patient he usually is with me. How he lets me say everything I'm feeling without making me feel ridiculous for it. How he reassures me over and over again, even when my fears don't make sense anymore.

And every time the storm finally settles, shame quietly takes its place.

Because none of it is really about him. He didn't do anything wrong. He doesn't deserve to be questioned for having a life before me, or for loving before me. Yet when jealousy takes over, I ask impossible questions anyway. How could he have given so much to someone else? Doesn't that make us less extraordinary?

Reading those words now, they sound almost absurd.

Because when my mind is calm, I know the answer already.

Nothing about us is ordinary.

My past has never really mattered to me. What matters is now. What matters is him, who listens to me before trying to fix me. Him, who asks what I think before deciding anything. Him, who makes room for every emotion I have instead of asking me to make them smaller.

I've never felt so respected by someone.

Respected because I am not turned down and told I'm less than anything because I'm talking.
Respected because I'm allowed to be imperfect without fearing that love will disappear.

That's probably the most beautiful gift anyone has ever given me.

Yesterday, I saw something I don't think I had fully seen before. I saw how much this hurts him too.

Not the jealousy itself. I've always known that part. I saw what comes after.

The guilt. The shame. The feeling that maybe you've become someone you never wanted to be.

And that broke my heart.

Because that's not what I saw when I looked at him.

I didn't see someone cruel. I didn't see someone impossible to love. I didn't see someone I should run away from.

I saw the person I love trying to fight thoughts that hurt him just as much as they hurt me.

I saw someone exhausted from arguing with his own mind. And I wished I could have carried some of it for you.

I don't only want to be next to him when everything is easy. I want to be there for this too.
For the ugly conversations. The irrational fears. The moments where neither of us likes what our own mind is doing.

Because loving you isn't just loving the version of you that laughs and teases me.

It's loving the version that hurts.

And I do.

Every single one of them.

So amor, if you ever read this one day...

Please don't blame yourself for yesterday.

You don't have to apologize for feeling deeply when I know exactly what that feels like. We both carry wounds that sometimes speak louder than reality. That doesn't make you difficult to love.

It just means we'll have to keep choosing each other while they heal.

And I will.

No amount of fear, jealousy or bad days could ever make me turn my back on you.
I love you on the easy days.
I love you on the impossible ones.
And I'll still be here when tomorrow comes.
