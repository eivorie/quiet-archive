2026.04.17

Today feels heavier than usual.

Not for me. For him.

I won’t go into details. I don’t need to. I can feel it anyway — the weight of it, sitting on his shoulders.

These past few days -months, even- have been rough for him. Complicated things. Things that drain you even when you don’t talk about them.

And I hate that I can’t do more.

I’m here. But I’m not really there.

France and America feel a lot further apart on days like this.

I listen. I stay. I try to be what he needs.

But it feels like it’s not enough. Like I’m watching him carry something heavy with empty hands. And I feel… useless.

There’s also something else I have to face. A feeling I didn’t really know before him.

Jealousy.

Not the kind that makes sense. Not the kind I’m proud of.

It’s not about the situation itself, the facts. It’s everything around it. What it represents. Why it exists at all.

And I hate that my mind goes there.

Because I know better.

I know him.

Still, I have to learn how to deal with it. Not ignore it — understand it.

This is new for me.

All of this is.

He brought something back to life in me that I don’t think was ever truly lit before.

And with it comes things I’ve never had to face.

Feelings that are stronger, louder, deeper than anything I knew.

I’m learning. Even when it’s uncomfortable. Even when it makes me question myself.

I just wish I could do more for him.

Not just be there. Actually help. Carry some of it with him.

Be a real partner in the way I want to be.

Give him everything I know I have in me.

He never makes me feel like I’m lacking.

Even now, when I know he’s exhausted.

He tells me it’s enough. That having me there, being able to talk to me — good or bad — is already what he needs.

He says that even if everything goes wrong, all he has to do is call me.

And somehow, that makes it okay for him.

I don’t think he realizes what that means to me.

But still…

I wish I could do more.
So much more.
