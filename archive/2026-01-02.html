Time moves strangely without you.
Too slow, too heavy, too empty around the edges.
When you’re sleeping, the world feels quiet in a way that doesn’t comfort me.
It just stretches — long, cold, and unbearably still.

I miss you in a way I don’t fully understand.
It’s not just longing.
It’s not just wanting.
It’s something deeper, sharper —
an ache that settles under my ribs and refuses to ease.

It’s ridiculous, isn’t it?
How someone can be only a few hours away, asleep,
and still leave me feeling like I’m reaching through the dark for something warm.
My chest feels too tight for reasons I can’t explain.
You’re not gone.
But the silence still hurts.

I keep catching myself reaching for you —
wanting to send something,
to say something,
to pull you closer in any way I can.
It feels instinctive now, like breathing.
My love keeps spilling out even when there’s nowhere for it to go.

You make me strong.
Stronger than I ever thought I could be.
But at the same time, you undo me so easily.
I don’t know how you manage to hold both sides of me in your hands —
the part that stands tall,
and the part that softens, trembles, breaks.

Balance is a joke when it comes to loving you.
I live between two extremes:
the strength you give me
and the weakness you pull out of me without even trying.
And both feel right.
Both feel like home.

I crave you in ways I can’t dress up with pretty words.
Not the grand gestures —
just the simplest things.
Your hand in mine.
The weight of your head against my shoulder.
A kiss you’d give me without thinking.
The kind of closeness that quiets every part of me.

I ache for it.
For you.
For the smallest touch that would tell my body you’re real,
that you’re here,
that this love lives outside of screens and distance and time zones.

Maybe that’s what loving you really is —
carrying this beautiful, unbearable ache
that only disappears the moment you speak,
breathe,
exist near me again.

I miss you.
More than I know how to explain.
More than I ever meant to.
And loving you still feels like the most natural thing in the world.
