Another horrible night, followed by another horrible morning. I keep waking up to the same reality, hoping for something to have changed while I wasn't looking. But nothing has. He's still not here. No message, no voice, nothing. Just this silence that I still don't know what to do with.

I keep wanting to open WhatsApp. It's unbearable. I want to ask him how he is, whether he's sleeping, whether he's eating, whether he's okay. I want to tell him I love him. I want to hear from him, even if it's just a few words. But I'm terrified he'll block me there too, and then there will be no place left where I can reach him. More than that, I'm terrified of disrespecting the one thing he actually asked of me: to leave him alone. And somehow, even now, when every part of me is screaming to reach out, I'm trying to give him that.

The longer he's away, the more paranoid I become. Every silence starts to mean something. Every passing hour gives my mind another opportunity to imagine things I don't want to imagine. I have no idea what he's thinking, what he's feeling, or whether he misses me at all. And not knowing is slowly eating away at me.

Yesterday, I spent most of the day somewhere inside my own head. And for a little while, it was better there. I thought I could feel his hand reaching for mine. I thought I could feel his arms around me, almost as though he were holding me. I know none of it was real. I know he wasn't there. But I wanted it so badly that I let myself believe in those sensations for a moment. I tried so hard to surrender to the illusion, to find him somewhere in my thoughts, somewhere I could still reach him when I couldn't reach him anywhere else.

And I miss him. God, I miss him. Not just the conversations or the messages, but him. His voice, his laughter, the way he speaks to me, the smallest things I never imagined having to go without. Everything around me continues as usual, and I feel completely disconnected from it. I don't want distractions. I want the life we were talking about together. I want him.

I know I've made mistakes. I know there are things I should have handled differently, moments when I let my feelings get the better of me. I have to live with that knowledge. But more than anything, I wish we had found a way to understand each other before reaching this point.

There are so many things I want to tell him. That I'm sorry. That I love him. That I hope he's okay. That I would give anything to hear his voice again. But I know he asked for space, and I don't want my need to hear from him to become another reason for him to pull away. So I keep those words here instead.

Yesterday, I kept telling myself he'd come back. I built a little world around that thought because, for a few hours, it was the only place where I could breathe. Maybe he will. Maybe he won't. I don't know. I just know that I'll never be ready to imagine a world where he doesn't.

It's another day without him. And somehow, the absence feels even bigger than it did yesterday.
