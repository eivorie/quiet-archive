2026.06.22

We’re not together.

Not really. Not on paper.

But nothing about this feels like we’re actually apart.

We still talk, every day. Like stopping would be worse than anything else.

We both know why we’re here.

Too much intensity. Too many reactions on impulse. Too much fear dressed as control.

The fear of losing. The fear of not being enough. The fear that giving space means losing everything.

So we said we needed time.

Time to think. Time to breathe. Time to stop hurting each other without meaning to.

I understand it.

I understand what he sees in me. I understand what I need to fix.

But understanding doesn’t make it easier.

There isn’t a moment in my day where he isn’t there. Everything still revolves around him. My thoughts. My nights. Life.

And I don’t want that to change.

The hardest part is holding back. Watching what I say. Making sure I don’t show too much.

Like loving him has to be controlled now.

It doesn’t feel natural. None of this does.

I try to convince myself we’re just starting over. That we’re back at the beginning. Learning each other again. Lighter. Slower.

My heart will never be “available” again.

It hasn’t been since the moment he said “hi.”

No matter what we are. Together or not.

There’s no version of my life where he isn’t in it.

I don’t learn how to live without him.

Never.

I don’t step back to let go. I step back so I don’t break what we have.

Even if it means holding everything in for a while.

It doesn’t change anything. Not the way I feel. Not the way I see him. Not the place he has in my life.
Time won’t take that away.

Because us — we make sense.

We always have.

And that’s not something that gets questioned.

Not now. Not ever. Not because things got harder.

If anything, this is just… us trying to remember why we chose each other in the first place.

Not fighting over things that don’t matter.

Just finding our way back to what does.
