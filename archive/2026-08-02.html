"Hi my dear! How have you been ♥"

Seven words. At the time, they were just a text. A simple greeting from someone I technically barely knew...

...and yet someone I'd already spent an embarrassingly long time thinking about after he'd mysteriously disappeared a few months earlier.

Looking back, I suppose being ghosted didn't work nearly as well as you expected.

I certainly wasn't expecting you to come back.
...I'm endlessly grateful that you did.

Nothing about that message suggested that, one year later, I'd be reading those words with a smile and feel my heart swell.

Today marks one year, sunshine.
The first out of countless more.

It's strange how a single conversation can quietly redraw the shape of an entire life.
One year ago today, you sent the first message.

Neither of us knew what would come from it. We couldn't have.

How could two strangers possibly imagine that, a year later, we'd still be here?
That we'd become part of each other's every day.
That your name would be the first thing I look for in the morning and the last thing I think about before falling asleep.

I used to think love needed grand moments. A beginning you could point to. A day that changed everything.

Looking back now... I don't think ours ever had one.

There wasn't an epiphany and I suddenly realized I loved you, no. It felt less like a realization and more like something my heart had known for a long time.
You simply became part of my life so naturally that somewhere along the way, imagining a day without you stopped making sense.

Seeing your name appear on my phone still feels like waking up on Christmas morning when you're five years old.
Like discovering Santa really did eat half the cookie, spilled milk all over the table -the bastard, and left something magical under the tree while you were asleep.

It never stops feeling that way. You are the gift.

You're my person. As much as I'm yours.

You belong with me. Just as much as I belong with you.

There is a kind of peace that only you is capable of giving me.

Do you even realize you have the gentlest heart I've ever known ?

Your kindness isn't something you perform. It's simply who you are.

The way you care.
The way you notice things everyone else overlooks.
The way you always try to understand before judging.

The way you love with your whole heart.

Sometimes I still struggle to believe you exist.
Yesterday, you didn't just write me beautiful words... You read them to me.
In that impossibly gentle voice of yours. The one that somehow quiets every anxious thought before I even realize I'm holding on to it.

By the time you'd finished, I couldn't find a single thing to say. I just kept thinking the same thing I always do...

How did someone as extraordinary as you ever choose me?

No one deserves a heart as kind as yours.
Maybe meeting you was simply the greatest gift life could have placed in my hands.
And I'll spend the rest of my life taking care of it.

You make the ordinary feel extraordinary.

A phone call. A story. A video of you walking somewhere. Watching football together from different continents. Laughing about nonsense things we see. Listening to you ramble while walking through a park.

All of those moments are remarkable.

They make me love you even more, if that is even possible.

You often thank me for being here but the truth is, I'm the one who should be thanking you.
Thank you for your patience. For your gentleness. For your empathy. For your ridiculous sense of humor. For making me laugh on days when I wanted nothing more than to disappear. For reminding me that love doesn't have to be loud to change someone's life.

Most of all...

Thank you for choosing me, every day.

One year ago, you started a conversation.

Without knowing it...

You also gave me a place where my heart finally feels at home.

Now I keep wondering what these letters will look like in five years.

Maybe I'll be writing the next one while you're still asleep beside me.

Maybe I'll stop halfway through because you'll decide my shoulder is a better pillow than yours.

Maybe I'll spend more time looking at you, handsome, than actually writing.

Maybe you'll wake up because I can't stop smiling at my screen while pretending to write in secret.

Maybe you'll steal the page before I'm finished.

Maybe we'll argue over who has to make breakfast (spoiler alert : that's on me).

Maybe we'll spend the afternoon doing absolutely nothing fancy but... I already know it'll be my favorite anniversary.

Exactly the kind of life I've always wanted with you.

Because it won't be another year spent loving you from the other side of the world.

It'll simply be another ordinary day.

With your hand in mine.

I'd choose you again.

Every conversation.
Every sunrise.
Every lifetime.
I hope each beat of my heart reminds you just how deeply you are loved.

So, one year later... if I finally have to answer your question,

I'm better than I've ever been. Thank you for asking.

Thank you for coming back.

Because I found home. In you.

Happy anniversary, meu amor.
