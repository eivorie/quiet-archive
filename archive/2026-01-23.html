Everything feels okay lately. Not perfect, not effortless — just okay in a way that feels strangely steady. I keep noticing how, whenever life gets heavy or exhausting, my mind goes straight to you. It’s not even a decision — it’s just where I end up, every time.

I’ve always been the kind of person who stays awake when I’m tired inside. My mind never really shuts down at night; it lingers, wanders, keeps me up. Four or five hours of sleep at best, never all at once. Being awake felt safer than dealing with the quiet.

But lately… I fall asleep. Not for long, but I do. Even when I want to stay awake because time with you feels so limited, so precious. Even when I tell myself I’ll push through like I always have.

I think it’s because I feel safe with you in a way I’m still learning to understand. My body trusts you before my thoughts do. It relaxes in ways it never has — like it finally knows it’s allowed to rest. It’s strange, and good, and new.

And the calmer I feel with you, the more I want of you. It’s never enough. I always want a little more — more of your voice, more of your presence, more of that quiet feeling that everything is manageable when you’re around. Maybe that’s why I get weird sometimes: needing you, missing you, craving you, all at once.

And lately… the craving is worse. In a soft way, no, scratch that, a desperate one — even deeper. When I wake up, I don’t even reach for my phone first anymore. My hand goes straight to the empty space beside me, like I expect you to be there before I’m fully conscious. Maybe I dream of you without remembering anything. Maybe it’s just the next logical thing when my mind circles around you all day. I just know I need your physical presence more and more lately. Being long distance is hard — we talk about it a lot — and I try not to let that longing spill out too much. But I’d give anything to wake you up by brushing the back of your hairline with my fingertips. Something simple. Something real. A kind of yearning that settles in the bones.

And I know you sometimes feel like I’m more attached when you’re working or asleep than when you’re fully here with me. Like I don’t show love the same way in those moments. But I do — I really do.

The thing is… when you stay awake just to be with me, I get worried. Worried you’ll get sick, or overtired, or push yourself too far just because you don’t want to leave me alone. I don’t ever want you to feel like you owe me every minute of your energy.

So when I tell you to sleep, or when I get a little quieter, it’s not distance. It’s not pulling away. It’s me making sure you’re okay — protecting the person I love in the only gentle way I know how.

You’re free, always. I want you to rest, to breathe, to enjoy your time without thinking that I’m waiting with some invisible expectation. But you never take that space — and that’s where we’re the same. Neither of us knows how to want less.

You make everything softer. Even the exhausting days. Even my own mind.

I love you in ways that keep growing — quiet, steady, completely. And if sleep finds me more easily now, it’s only because loving you feels safe. Because you feel like home.

I’m still learning how to show what I feel, still figuring out how to love you the way you deserve. But I want to keep growing with you — every day, in every small way. You deserve the world, and I want to become someone who can give you all of it, piece by piece, with everything I have.
