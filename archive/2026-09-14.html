I woke up. I thought about him.

I told him good morning, like I always do.

I washed my face. I thought about him.

I did my Portuguese lesson. I thought about him.

I scrolled through Instagram. I thought about him.

I made myself something to eat. I thought about him.

Then I realized that it doesn't matter what I'm doing.

Even when I'm busy, I finish whatever I'm doing almost without thinking.

My mind doesn't even have to be free.

It goes back to him. Every single time.

Today feels no different.

The highs and the lows are still there, but underneath all of them, nothing has changed.

I'm still completely incapable without him.

That's probably the most alarming conclusion I've reached.

I don't know what he did to me.

Maybe it's the feeling of safety.

Maybe it's knowing that no matter what happens, he's on my side.

My ally. My soulmate.

I just need him with me.

And somehow...

The longer this goes on, the more physical it becomes.

I keep thinking that, one day, I'll finally feel his skin.

I'll hear his voice without thousands of kilometers standing between us.

I'll finally be able to love him in all the ways distance never allowed.

Even knowing that I'll see him one day doesn't calm me.

I don't know how I'm supposed to survive that either.

The thought alone burns.

I still haven't gotten used to his "I love you."

Or his affection.

Or even seeing his name light up on my phone.

It's always the same.

The same rush.

The same knot in my stomach.

The same excitement.

The same need for just a little more of him.

I know it sounds unhealthy, yet it still feels more right than anything else.

I've stopped wondering whether it's too much. I'm just grateful I get to love him at all.

September 19th is almost here.

One year since the confession. It sounds dramatic when I write it like that.

But if simply meeting him turned my entire life upside down...

Imagine what happened the day he told me he loved me.

We've almost made it to one whole year.

And I'm so proud of us.

I'm so unbelievably proud of him.

And I love him even more as days go.

I miss him.

I miss him all the time.

I honestly think this has become some kind of illness.

This weekend can't come soon enough.

We both planned to spend it together.

Even from opposite sides of the ocean.

I can't wait to have him all to myself for one entire day...

And one entire night.

If I'm allowed one single fear...

It's that loving him this much will only make me miss him even more.
