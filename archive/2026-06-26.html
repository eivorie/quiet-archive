These past few days have been... quiet, in the nicest way.

After everything that happened, I didn't expect quiet to feel this comforting.
I thought silence would scare me, that every conversation would carry the weight of what came before. Instead, we've somehow found our way back to something lighter. We laugh. We watch things together. We sit through a movie without thinking about anything except the fact that we're sharing those couple of hours.

I missed that.

Not because we had lost it forever, but because I'd forgotten how beautiful it is to simply enjoy his company. No questions. No overthinking. Just the two of us, and whatever we're watching while making stupid comments to each other.

He probably doesn't even realize how much the little things stay with me.

Yesterday morning, I noticed he had liked a reel on Instagram. It was talking about what genuine love feels like. About how, after spending so much time in relationships where you constantly had to protect yourself... being accepted exactly as they are can almost feel unfamiliar. Almost suspicious.

It was just a like.

Something anyone else would scroll past without thinking twice.

But I stared at it for a while.

It moved me more than I know how to explain. Maybe because I wondered what had gone through his mind when he did. Or maybe because it reminded me that love isn't always grand declarations. Sometimes it's finding each other in the smallest gestures, without either of us saying a word. Maybe because, for once, I let myself believe it was about us.

He's asleep as I'm writing this.

And I already miss him.

It's strange trying to explain how much I miss him, because it isn't the kind of missing I knew before. It isn't loud or dramatic. It sits somewhere much deeper than that. Like a quiet certainty that something is missing from the room, even when everything else seems exactly where it should be.
Okay, maybe it is dramatic. Sheesh.

I keep imagining simple things.

Sitting next to him while he sleeps, gently playing with his hair until he wakes up. Making breakfast before he opens his eyes. Hearing him shuffle through the apartment while I pretend not to notice he's stolen something from the pan before it's ready.

A life.

Just... a life with him.

Yesterday he kept turning his phone around to show me where he was. The streets. The restaurant he wanted to eat at. The places he liked. He probably thought he was only showing me a few buildings and a few plates.
But for a few minutes, I wasn't watching a video.

I was walking beside him.

I closed my eyes afterwards and realized I've been doing that a lot lately. Not to escape reality, but to imagine one that's getting closer every day. One where there isn't a screen between us anymore. One where I don't have to imagine reaching for his hand because it'll already be there.
His fingers intertwined with mine, like they always should have been.

Maybe that's what loving someone with everything you have actually feels like.

Sometimes it's just wanting to make breakfast for the person who's still asleep in the next room.

Now, before I start tearing up again... let's just hope France remembers how to play football tonight against Norway, otherwise I might have to take back everything I just said about these peaceful days.
We're gonna have our France - Brasil.
Com certeza, amorzinho.
