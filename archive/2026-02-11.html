I get bored out of my mind when he’s not here. Not the cute “I miss him” kind — the real kind. The kind where every minute drags, everything feels pointless, and I swear I can feel my hair growing because time is moving so damn slow.

I try to be normal about it. “He’s sleeping, it’s fine, go do something else.” Yeah. No. Nothing hits the same when he’s not around. Everything loses its flavor, its spark, its rhythm. It’s like my whole brain goes into standby mode waiting for one single person to come back online.

And honestly? I don't get used to it. I never do. I can talk big, act chill, pretend I’m busy — but the truth is stupidly simple: he’s missing, so everything is off. Even the silence feels wrong.

It’s crazy how someone can fill a space just by existing. Not even talking — just being there. When he’s here, everything makes sense without trying. When he’s not, nothing does. I hate it, and I love it, and I wouldn’t trade it for anything. That’s the part that gets me every time.

So I wait. Not patiently — I don’t have that skill. I wait the way people wait for something that actually matters. And the moment he comes back, even with a simple “hey”, the whole world snaps back into place. Just like that. And I can breathe again.

It’s ridiculous. I know it. I’ll complain about it again tomorrow. And I’ll still love him like he’s the only source of oxygen on Earth.
He kind of is.
