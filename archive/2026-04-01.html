April 1st.
And somehow, I keep thinking about tomorrow.

Maybe I'm being dramatic, too much, but it means something. Any hour counts, any minute is blessed, any second matters.

Tomorrow makes it eight months. Eight months that don’t feel like eight months at all.
It feels longer than that.
Not in time — but in depth.

It feels like knowing someone for years.
Like skipping something most people go through.

There was no phase of “getting to know each other.”
No careful steps, no distance, no slow build.

It just happened, the way it was supposed to.

Things were understood before they were explained.
Reactions came before context.
Expectations existed without ever being discussed.

I was his, before he could even think of it.

A week after his first message, he was already mad at me.
Not slightly — completely.
Enough to react, enough to feel, enough to lose control over something that barely had a name yet.

And he didn’t even know my full name.

That says everything about how this started, right ?

Nothing about this has ever been measured or cautious.
It’s intense in a way that doesn’t ask for permission.
It just takes all the space.

The way we talk reflects that.
There’s no filtering, no holding back, no calculated version of anything.

Everything is shared.
Everything is said.
Not only because it’s expected — but it feels natural to do it.

Like there’s no point in hiding anything.
Like nothing is worth keeping to myself anymore.

At least… that’s how it is for me.

There’s no shame attached to anything when it comes to him.
No hesitation in saying things exactly as they are.
No instinct to protect parts of myself by keeping them hidden.

It’s the opposite, actually.

It feels like everything can be given.
Freely, entirely, without calculation.

And maybe that’s what makes this so unsettling sometimes.

Because nothing about it feels partial.
Nothing feels light or temporary.
It’s either everything, or nothing at all.

He is everything.

Last year, there was already something missing.
A space that didn’t make sense at the time.
A craving I could not understand.

And now it does.

Now that absence has more than a shape.
A voice.
A presence that became necessary without ever asking to be.

It’s strange to think about how quickly that shift happened.
How naturally it settled into something that feels permanent, like home.

Because now, even something as small as an hour without him feels different.
And I can't help but run to him as soon as he says "hi".

So... eight months.
And it still doesn’t feel like the beginning of something.
It feels like something that was already there,
just finally found.
And yet, somehow, everything is still new.
I discover something about him every day.
The way he thinks, the way he reacts, the way he feels — it never settles into something predictable.

It’s not something everyone gets to experience,
having someone like him in their life.
Someone who keeps surprising you, even with what seems to be the smallest casual thing.
There’s something to learn from him.
In the way he exists, in the way he feels things so intensely,
in the way nothing about him is ever halfway.

And maybe that’s why it never feels still.
Maybe that's why I can't seem to stop loving him more everyday.
Forever, amor. Always.
