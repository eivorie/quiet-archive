I've never been under any illusion. I know exactly how much space he occupies in my life. I just never imagined what that space would feel like when it suddenly went quiet.

It's unbearable.

I don't know if he's eaten. I don't know if he went outside. I don't know if he got home safely. I don't know how he's feeling. I don't know if he needs anything.

The only thing I know...

is that he had breakfast.

That's it.

The only reason I know he had breakfast is because he posted a story.

I wanted him to have a good day.

I wanted him to forget I existed, if only for a few hours.

I zoomed in on my notifications until I was practically squinting, just to see what the story was.

What song he'd used.

Anything... without letting him see I'd watched it.

Then I went back to bed.

And now that I'm writing it down...

I realize how ridiculous I must look.

Trying so hard to disappear, while secretly looking for proof that he's okay.

I keep walking from one room to another, doing absolutely nothing.

Time refuses to move.

Every few minutes I look at the clock, convinced an hour must have passed.

It never has.

I'm doing my best to stay away.

I'm starting to feel like I'm really losing my mind.

What frightens me most is that I still can't make a decision.

A decision that, with anyone else, would've been effortless.

I wouldn't even have questioned it.

I'd have closed the chapter.

Turned the page.

Kept walking.

But this isn't anyone else.

It's him.

That changes everything.

I'll never know what he does to me.

I just know that nobody else ever did.

I'm obsessed with the guy. The guy who said he didn't want me to leave.
