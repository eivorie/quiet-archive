Chapter VI

Eren

Eren was in his room when he was suddenly hit by a ray of sunlight directly in the face. He squinted his eyes.

“Are you going to sleep all day? We have to start setting up the stalls, and there’s a tournament happening here today. A day for celebration, food, and making a little money. Don’t be lazy…” Carla finished opening the window and noticed the scar on Eren, who was shirtless.

Eren noticed her gaze and didn’t wait for her to ask.

“It happened at the camp. I really don’t do well on trails. I got this little scratch as a souvenir.” He smiled.

Carla smiled back. “You’re a little distracted, to put it mildly… Should I get some arnica for you?”

“No, no, I already used some. I’ll get some ice. Arnica alone isn’t going to fix this.” He said, getting out of bed.

“Alright…” she said, studying Eren for a little longer before leaving.

After some time, Eren got dressed and went outside, walking through the village. Everyone was busy setting things up, hanging the torches, and preparing everything. They all seemed excited, but Eren remained concerned.

A few flashes from the previous night crossed his mind, along with a little suspicion. A little apprehension. But for some reason, Levi’s eyes kept coming back to his mind, interfering with his thoughts.

“Eren!” Armin came over and hugged him. “How are you?” He pulled Eren toward a more secluded corner.

“I have a lot to tell you, but… I’m fine,” Eren said.

Armin noticed how anxious Eren was and decided not to push him too much.

“We’re going to talk a lot tonight, with a drink in our hands. It’s alright. It seems Zeke is satisfied with the last harvest and hasn’t questioned anything else so far. We’re okay…”

Eren took a deep breath, but his mind still refused to shut off. Yet, seeing Armin’s enthusiasm, he decided to let it go.

“Let’s just enjoy the day. It’s the most we can do.” He said, though he didn’t even manage a smile.

Levi

Levi had managed perhaps two hours of sleep. It wasn't enough, but it would have to be.

By late morning, he was already crossing the castle courtyard again, freshly changed, the marks around his wrists hidden beneath his gloves and a considerably stronger cup of tea doing what sleep had failed to accomplish.

The missing soldiers had confirmed Hanji's account. Neither could identify their attackers. Both remembered being ambushed near the southern fields, waking somewhere dark, and eventually being left near the eastern road.

Convenient.

Levi had asked whether either of them had seen Eren. Neither had. Even more convenient. He stopped thinking about it before irritation ruined the little rest he'd managed to get.

The village tournament had complicated matters further. Cancelling it would cause panic over an incident the Crown couldn't yet explain, and hundreds of people were already expected to attend. Instead, several soldiers had been assigned to the village for the day.

Levi had volunteered before anyone could assign someone else. Needless to say, he preferred not to examine that decision too closely.

Security was a perfectly reasonable explanation.

The fact that Eren Jaeger happened to live there was irrelevant. Mostly.

By the time the village came into view, preparations were already well underway. Stalls lined the streets, ribbons and lanterns hung between buildings, and the smell of bread, roasted meat and something sweet drifted through the morning air. Children ran between adults carrying crates while merchants argued over spaces that had apparently belonged to their families since the beginning of civilization.

Levi slowed his horse.

This was different from the camp.

There, the villagers had been trainees. Representatives. People gathered temporarily under the supervision of the Crown.

Here, this was their world.

And somewhere inside it was a man who had apparently managed to become the leader of a group stealing from royal estates while still finding time to sell medicinal herbs and pretend he couldn't shoot a bow.

Levi dismounted near the central square.

“Captain.”

One of the guards approached.

“We've been told to keep the eastern and southern entrances covered. The tournament starts this afternoon. Nothing unusual so far.”

“Keep it that way. Don't start searching people without cause. We're here to prevent trouble, not create it.”

The soldier nodded and moved away.

Levi adjusted his gloves. He should inspect the perimeter, speak with the local guards, check the roads. Instead, his attention wandered across the square.

Once.

Then again.

Annoying.

He knew exactly who he was looking for. And apparently the universe had decided not to make him wait long.

A familiar figure stood farther down the street beside one of the stalls, partially obscured by people carrying baskets and wooden crates.

There he was.

Levi stopped.

For a moment, neither the theft nor the hideout came first. His eyes went automatically to Eren's side. The ribs.

He remembered the wince from the night before, the cotton pressed against bruised skin and the ridiculous satisfaction he'd felt seeing Eren had apparently listened to him about the ice.

Then common sense returned. Levi's expression flattened.

Don't start.

He had spent half the night wondering whether Eren had lied about the soldiers. He wasn't going to forget that because the idiot had followed one piece of medical advice.

Still...

Eren was here, walking around freely. Alive.

Levi hadn't realized until that moment that some irritating part of him had needed to confirm it.

He started toward the square.

Not directly toward Eren, that would be ridiculous.

He had work to do.

Unfortunately, that work appeared to be taking him in precisely the same direction.
