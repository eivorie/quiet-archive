Chapter IX

Eren

Eren said nothing; he merely watched Levi give the order concerning Floch. The guard withdrew, and Eren swallowed hard. Levi was examining his wrist, Eren’s eyes remaining on Levi’s face until he felt Levi’s gaze meet his own, prompting him to quickly look away and down at his wrists.

He listened attentively to Levi until he watched him rise, and then he reached out and firmly grabbed his arm.

— Wait…

Eren released Levi’s arm when he noticed him yield, lowering himself back into the same position. Eren’s head remained resting against the wall, and only his gaze shifted toward Levi.

— I know you’re exhausted, and believe me, so am I… —Eren sighed softly but kept his eyes on Levi.

— Your concern… — He paused, reconsidering his words, but continued. — Does your concern for Petra go beyond the duties of your squad?

He noticed the confused look in Levi’s eyes, but before he could open his mouth, Eren cut him off.

— Just answer me, please.

Levi

Levi looked down at the hand around his arm.

He could have pulled away. Instead, when Eren released him, he stayed where he was.

The question wasn't what he'd expected.

— No.

There was no hesitation.

— Petra is one of my soldiers. She's under my command, and that makes her my responsibility. That's all.

Levi studied Eren for another second.

— Now explain to me why that matters.

His expression hardened before Eren could turn the question into another mystery.

— Actually, don't.

He was tired of this.

— Whatever you're doing with Petra, whatever purpose she's supposed to serve, stop treating people like pieces on a board.

Levi lowered himself slightly again, enough that Eren didn't have to look up at him.

— You keep asking me to understand your people. To trust that there's a reason behind everything you do. Fine. I've seen enough to know you're not doing all of this for the pleasure of causing trouble.

That was perhaps more credit than Levi had given him aloud until now.

— But you don't get to demand that I see the value in every life you're protecting while acting as though mine only matter when they're useful to whatever plan you're running.

His eyes stayed on Eren's.

— That's what I'm done with.

A brief silence passed.

— And the stupidest part is that I don't think we're nearly as far apart as you seem determined to make us.

Levi had seen Ashvale. He'd seen what the villagers lacked, what the Crown ignored and what Eren was trying to keep alive despite it. None of that required blind obedience to understand.

— You want to protect your people. So do I. That should be the simplest fucking thing between us.

His jaw tightened.

— Instead, every time I think we're getting somewhere, you arrange another little game behind my back and somebody ends up tied up, missing or sitting in a cell.

Levi straightened.

— So no. I'm not in love with Petra, if that's what you're trying to establish.

Dry irritation slipped into his voice.

— I shouldn't have to be for her life to matter.

His gaze remained on Eren.

— Neither should you.

Eren

Eren kept his head resting against the wall and swallowed hard after hearing Levi, but his eyes never left him.

— I need you to trust me, and yes, I know it’s not easy for you, but it’s the only way to bring Petra back safe and sound.

Eren glanced around as if making sure there was no one nearby, and then, looking Levi in the eyes, he spoke in a low voice.

— I need you to let me out. You have two options. You let me go, and as a guarantee, you’ll find my key among the belongings your guards took from us. That key opens the hideout in the tavern. I wouldn’t give it to you if I weren’t certain I’d come back for it. The second option is that you come with me, but I need you to wait until we reach a certain point. They can’t see that you’re with me, or I can guarantee you we won’t be leaving there in good shape.

Eren held Levi’s gaze a little longer, his eyes serious.

— Levi, I’m the only one who can kill Petra or bring her back.

Levi

Levi stared at him. For several seconds, he genuinely said nothing.

Then he exhaled through his nose.

— Option three.

He crouched again, forearms resting loosely against his thighs.

— You stay exactly where you are and, for once in your life, follow a simple fucking instruction.

His voice wasn't raised. If anything, he sounded tired.

— Let me make sure I understand what you just offered me. I release a prisoner who has now openly admitted that Petra's life depends on him, and I trust that he'll wander back because he left me a key.

A pause.

— Or I leave the castle with that same prisoner, secretly follow him somewhere his associates can't know I'm going, wait wherever he tells me to wait and trust that this time I won't wake up drugged and tied to something. Brilliant choices.

The key, at least, was something. Eren wouldn't surrender access to the tavern lightly, and Levi knew it. He filed that away without allowing it to change the answer.

— No. You're staying here.

Levi held his gaze.

— And you're going to tell me what needs to happen for Petra to come back. Names aren't necessary. I don't need the location of your hideouts. I don't need you to hand me every person involved. If there's something I need to do, tell me what it is. If there's something I need to stop doing, tell me that.

He leaned a fraction closer.

— But I'm not handing you the keys to your cell every time you tell me somebody might die if I don't obey.

Then Eren's final words settled properly.

"I'm the only one who can kill Petra or bring her back."

Levi went very still.

— And you should probably think very carefully before you phrase anything like that again.

There was no threat in his voice. Just exhaustion now, and something considerably colder underneath it.

— Because I'm trying very hard to believe that Petra is alive because you arranged for her to be kept that way.

His eyes stayed fixed on Eren's.

— Don't make me start wondering whether she's alive because you decided she was useful that way.

Levi straightened.

— You want me to trust you? Start with something smaller than asking me to put my life, my soldier and half the fucking castle in your hands again.

He glanced toward the cell door.

— Tell me what you need. From here.

Eren

Eren listened to everything attentively. He felt the urge to interrupt Levi, but he didn’t. He kept looking into Levi’s eyes, his voice low, just as exhausted.


— I know... I didn’t expect you to make this easy. But the thing is, Levi, I can give you all the directions, the artifacts you’ll need, and the best route through the forest you could possibly take. The reality is, they’re forbidden from handing Petra over without me being there.


Eren was concise as he spoke, slightly shifting his wrists, which were still sore, as he glanced down at them.


—You have complete control over my allies here. I wouldn’t be stupid enough to gamble with their lives.

He sighed and extended his wrists, held together, toward Levi.

—You can tie me up again and keep me here, locked away, without answers, with Petra’s life left at the mercy of whatever dangers await her... or you can choose to trust me for once.

Levi

Levi's eyes dropped to the wrists Eren held out to him. For a moment, he didn't move. Then he took one of them.

His fingers settled carefully around Eren's forearm instead, turning his hand just enough to examine the swelling without putting pressure on the inflamed skin. His expression tightened.

— Stop moving these around.

His thumb passed lightly beside the worst of the irritation, checking the heat there.

— I'm not tying anything over this again until the swelling goes down.

Only then did Levi look back at him.

— Now, as for trusting you 'for once'.

Something in his face went flat.

— I followed you into that forest because you asked me to trust you. I woke up drugged and tied to a chair.

His hold on Eren's wrist remained careful.

— I gave you the benefit of the doubt after that. I kept what I learned to myself. I didn't hand your name to the Crown. And somehow we've ended up here, with another one of my people missing because of a plan you neglected to mention.

Levi released his wrist.

— So don't stand there and talk to me like trust is something I haven't tried giving you.

He rose, irritation finally showing through the exhaustion.

— You've had it. Twice. What you did with it was your decision.

The problem was that none of that changed Petra's situation. He hated that more than anything.

Eren had constructed the entire thing so that refusing him meant leaving Petra wherever she'd been taken. Whether that had been intentional or merely another consequence of whatever system his people operated under hardly mattered now.

— I'm not letting you out because I trust you.

He made the distinction deliberately.

— I'm taking you because Petra doesn't deserve to pay for the fact that you've made yourself indispensable to getting her back.

His gaze sharpened.

— You don't leave my sight unless the situation requires it. You don't change the route without telling me why. You don't improvise another one of your clever little plans and expect me to discover it afterward.

Levi stepped closer again.

— And if you know there's a point where I can't follow you, you tell me before we reach it. Not when we're already standing there.

A pause.

— Those are my terms.

He glanced once more at Eren's wrists.

— And we're dealing with those before you go anywhere.

Then his eyes returned to Eren's.

— You asked me to trust you again.

Levi held his gaze for a long moment.

— Fine.

The word tasted bitter.

— But understand something before I open that door.

His expression went perfectly still.

— There won't be a third time.

Eren

Eren never stopped looking at Levi, not while he touched his wrist, not while he laid down his orders.

— I agree to all the rules you're asking for, and they will be followed. You'll know the routes, the paths that pose any danger, and any interference along the way. If anything happens, I won't let anything happen to you. That's something I can promise you.
He sighed softly.

— I think you should know that you have more control than I do at the moment. You practically have my entire squad here, and there's one more thing, Levi… Soon, this will no longer be about whether you give them names or not. The Crown will demand that the cells be cleared out. I'd rather that happen with you placing at least a little trust in me.

He reached out and touched one of the knives tucked into Levi's belt, which was about to fall loose. He secured it back into place before lifting his eyes to Levi's again.

— We'll need some supplies, horses in good condition, and some equipment. I don't trust that there won't be soldiers stationed around the area to prevent anyone from passing through. Ah and get two black masks. We'll need them.

Levi

Levi glanced down at the knife Eren had pushed securely back into place, then at him.

For a moment, he didn't move.

It was such a stupidly familiar gesture. Eren's fingers near his belt, those green eyes lifting to meet his afterward as though the last few days hadn't put an entire damn wall between them.

Something in Levi's chest pulled unpleasantly. He stepped back before he could give it any attention.

— Anything else? Bag of gold? Spare horse in case you dislike the first one?

The sarcasm came easily. Easier than everything else.

None of what Eren had asked for was particularly difficult to arrange. The timing was.

— Zeke arrives tomorrow morning. I can't disappear into the forest indefinitely while the castle is preparing for a diplomatic visit.

Levi folded his arms.

— How long from the moment we leave this castle until Petra is back with us? Give me an actual estimate.

He paused, studying Eren again.

He was tired of wondering which version of him he was looking at. The man who had warned him to keep a knife ready beneath the fireworks, or the one who kept deciding Levi could survive one more lie. The idiot who brushed dirt from his cheek, kissed him beside the lake, then somehow made every memory of it harder to trust afterward.

And despite all of that, Levi was about to open the door.

One last time.

— If this goes the way you're promising me it will, we'll talk about Connie when we get back.

His voice had lost some of its edge.

— Bring my squad home. Keep your word to me this time.

Levi held his gaze.

— Give me one reason to believe I wasn't an idiot for doing this again.
